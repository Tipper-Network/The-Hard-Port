# THP SMB Case Study and Proof System

> **Status:** `working` · Public proof requires separate permission from service delivery.

A completed deliverable is not proof of business transformation.

Public proof must identify the starting condition, intervention, evidence, limitations, and permission status.

## Proof levels (`proof_permission_level`)

| ID | Description |
|---|---|
| `internal_learning_record` | Restricted internal improvement — not public |
| `anonymous_evidence_note` | Pattern description without identifying the business |
| `approved_case_study` | Structured case study — requires `case_study_permission` |
| `public_media_story` | Named media use — requires `public_content_permission` and participant permissions |

---

## 11. Proof and Case-Study System

THP must define proof before producing marketing claims.

Every engagement should preserve:

## Starting condition

* Maturity classification.
* Founder beliefs.
* Primary symptoms.
* Available evidence.
* Level 2 gaps.
* Baseline conditions.

## Intervention record

* Constraint selected.
* Decisions made.
* Actions performed.
* Founder actions.
* THP actions.
* Execution services.
* Changes to scope.

## Outcome evidence

* Customer behavior.
* Repeat and referral behavior.
* Financial visibility.
* Operational consistency.
* Founder dependency.
* Decision quality.
* Identity clarity.
* Community participation.
* Level 2 gate movement.

## Contradictory evidence

The case record must preserve:

* Failed actions.
* Negative results.
* Customer disagreement.
* Implementation failure.
* Unchanged conditions.
* Unintended consequences.

## Limitations

THP must state:

* What cannot be attributed to the intervention.
* Which evidence remains weak.
* Which outcomes may be temporary.
* Which external conditions affected the business.
* What remains unresolved.

## 12. Proof Levels

Canonical IDs use `proof_permission_level`. Legacy labels in parentheses.

## `internal_learning_record` (legacy: Level 1 — Internal Learning Record)

Not public.

Used to improve:

* Diagnosis.
* Intervention.
* Maturity levels.
* Tipper hypotheses.
* Service delivery.

## `anonymous_evidence_note` (legacy: Level 2 — Anonymous Evidence Note)

Describes a pattern without identifying the business.

Example:

> An online microbusiness increased repeat customer visibility after replacing informal direct-message feedback with a structured post-delivery process.

This must not imply stronger evidence than exists.

## `approved_case_study` (legacy: Level 3 — Approved Case Study)

May include:

* Business context.
* Starting condition.
* Intervention.
* Evidence.
* Outcomes.
* Limitations.
* Founder perspective.

Requires case-study permission.

## `public_media_story` (legacy: Level 4 — Public Media Story)

May include:

* Founder interview.
* Business name.
* Visual material.
* Customer participation.
* Published metrics.
* YouTube or documentary content.

Requires explicit public-content permission and any additional participant permissions.

## 13. What Counts as Proof

Strong proof includes:

* Repeated customer behavior.
* Improved retention visibility.
* Increased operational consistency.
* Reduced founder reactivity.
* Better decision traceability.
* Clearer offer performance.
* Increased financial visibility.
* Level 2 gates sustained across business cycles.
* Customer or community evidence supporting the interpretation.

Weak proof includes:

* One viral post.
* Increased impressions.
* Founder confidence alone.
* One unusually strong sales week.
* A completed rebrand.
* A larger content volume.
* Testimonials without supporting behavior.
* Revenue claims without costs or context.

Weak proof may still be recorded.

It must not be presented as institutional validation.
