# Manual-First Tipper Boundary

> **Status:** `working` · Tipper is manual-first until the feature-development gate is satisfied across real entities.

## Feature-development gate

A consulting workflow becomes a Tipper product candidate only when:

1. The need appears across at least three real entities.
2. The same relationship or decision problem repeats.
3. The manual process produces useful behavior.
4. The required data structure is becoming stable.
5. Entity and community value are both visible.
6. Privacy and permission requirements are understood.
7. Software would materially improve the process.

Three entities are the **minimum trigger for product investigation** — not market validation and not automatic product building.

---

## 10. Manual-First Tipper Boundary

Tipper should not be built around untested consulting assumptions.

During the first engagements, THP should manually simulate the relationship and evidence systems that Tipper may eventually support.

## Manual systems to test

* Core-supporter register.
* Post-delivery follow-up.
* Feedback tickets.
* Customer questions.
* Suggestions.
* Repeat and referral records.
* Participation records.
* Community-pattern observations.
* Founder and THP Decision Records.
* Business-cycle evidence.
* Relationship history.

## What THP should observe

For each manual system:

* Does the founder use it?
* Do customers participate?
* Does it improve decisions?
* Does it reveal something existing tools do not?
* Does it strengthen customer relationships?
* Does it create unnecessary work?
* Which information is consistently required?
* Which permissions are needed?
* What becomes valuable only when multiple entities use it?

## Tipper feature-development gate

A feature should not move from manual process to product development until:

1. The need appears across at least three real entities.
2. The same relationship or decision problem repeats.
3. The manual process produces useful behavior.
4. The required data structure is becoming stable.
5. Entity and community value are both visible.
6. Permission and privacy requirements are understood.
7. Software would meaningfully improve the process.

Three clients do not prove a global market.

They provide the minimum evidence required to justify structured product investigation.

## Current Tipper role

During early THP engagements, Tipper is:

* A research destination.
* A hypothesis.
* A possible future infrastructure layer.
* A manual-system observation target.

It is not yet:

* A mandatory client deliverable.
* A condition for reaching Level 2.
* A replacement for customer conversations.
* A guaranteed community engine.
