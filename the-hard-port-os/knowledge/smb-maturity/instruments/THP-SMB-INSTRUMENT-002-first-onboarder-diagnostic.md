# First-Onboarder Diagnostic

> **Status:** `founder_input_required`, `canonicality: working` — not operational until structure, questions, and evidence gates are complete and tested.  
> **Active operational boundary:** Level 1 → Level 2. Level 3 is boundary context only.

# Purpose

Determine whether an SMB qualifies as THP's first-onboarder profile, classify its present maturity condition, identify the barriers preventing Level 2, and recommend the first THP intervention.

The diagnostic must produce a **structured conclusion**, not a vague score. Example:

> This is a Level 1 business. Its strongest area is demand evidence. Its main barriers to Level 2 are customer understanding, financial visibility, and founder dependency. The first intervention should clarify the core customer and offer before increasing content or acquisition activity.
