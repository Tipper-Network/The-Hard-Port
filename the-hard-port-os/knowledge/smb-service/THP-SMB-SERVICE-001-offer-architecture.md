# THP SMB Offer Architecture

> **Status:** `working` · **Not canonical.**  
> `field_validation: pending_first_entity` · `commercial_validation: pending` · `legal_validation: pending`

## Working Commercial Defaults

> **Working recommendation — not confirmed price policy or canonical commercial policy.**

- Discovery and application are free.
- Qualification is free during first-client validation.
- The diagnostic is paid and fixed-scope.
- Fifty percent of the diagnostic fee may be credited toward the intervention.
- The intervention uses milestone-based fixed fees.
- Optional execution services are explicitly included or separately priced.

Recommended intervention payment milestones (working default, not canonical): 40% before kickoff · 30% after Viable Core and Constraint Diagnosis approved · 30% before Level 2 Proof and final handoff.

---

## Business Reality Diagnostic

### Purpose

Determine:

* What business currently exists.
* Whether it is operating at Level 1.
* Which Level 2 conditions already exist.
* Which conditions are missing.
* What the primary constraint is.
* What THP should address first.
* What the business should not do yet.

### Core outputs

* Current-business map.
* Evidence inventory.
* Dimension findings across A–H.
* Level 1 classification.
* Level 2 gap assessment.
* Primary-constraint hypothesis.
* Immediate-risk register.
* Intervention recommendation.
* Findings presentation.

### Commercial position

The diagnostic is a paid professional service.

It must not be presented as:

* A free sales call.
* A social-media audit.
* A branding consultation.
* A content-strategy session.
* A guaranteed route to revenue.

The client may purchase the diagnostic without committing to the intervention.

### Recommended launch rule

A portion of the diagnostic fee may be credited toward the intervention when the client proceeds within the proposal-validity period.

Working recommendation:

> Credit 50% of the diagnostic fee toward the intervention.

This preserves the value of the diagnostic while reducing friction for clients who continue.

## Level 1-to-Level 2 Stability Intervention

### Purpose

Help the business establish the operating foundation required to move beyond survival and prepare for deliberate growth.

### Intervention phases

1. Business Reality.
2. Evidence Recovery.
3. Viable Core.
4. Constraint Diagnosis.
5. Identity Clarification.
6. Relationship Foundation.
7. Operating Foundation.
8. Level 2 Proof.

### Core institutional outputs

The intervention should produce, where relevant:

* A defined viable core.
* A clarified core customer.
* A clarified core offer.
* A delivered-value statement.
* An organized evidence system.
* A primary-constraint decision.
* A business identity and message foundation.
* A customer-feedback method.
* A relationship and community observation system.
* A repeatable core operating workflow.
* Responsibility and capacity boundaries.
* A recurring evidence and decision rhythm.
* A Level 2 reassessment.
* A continuation or completion recommendation.

### What THP promises

THP promises:

* A disciplined evidence-based process.
* Honest interpretation.
* Clear decisions.
* Defined operating and relationship systems.
* Progress measurement against Level 2 gates.
* Transparent uncertainty.
* A final maturity conclusion.

### What THP does not promise

THP does not promise:

* Guaranteed sales.
* Guaranteed profit.
* Virality.
* A specific follower count.
* Investment.
* Immediate growth.
* That every client will reach Level 2.
* That branding or content can repair a structurally weak business.
* That Tipper adoption automatically creates community.

## Optional Execution Services

Execution services exist inside the intervention. They are not the intervention itself.

## Identity and messaging execution

May include:

* Positioning refinement.
* Message hierarchy.
* Brand-language system.
* Visual identity work.
* Profile and website copy.
* Offer presentation.

## Content-system execution

May include:

* Content strategy.
* Content pillars.
* Publishing rhythm.
* Educational series.
* Customer-evidence content.
* Content production.
* Performance review.

Content must address a diagnosed constraint.

THP should never sell posting consistency as a substitute for business stability.

## Customer and community execution

May include:

* Customer interviews.
* Feedback collection.
* Post-delivery follow-up.
* Referral observation.
* Community questions.
* Participation mechanisms.
* Manual community records.

## Operating-system execution

May include:

* Workflow documentation.
* Responsibility mapping.
* Capacity rules.
* Delivery standards.
* Decision records.
* Evidence-review routines.
* Founder-knowledge externalization.

## Digital-presence execution

May include:

* Website corrections.
* Social-profile restructuring.
* Customer journey improvements.
* Offer pages.
* Forms.
* Feedback tools.
* Basic reporting infrastructure.

Each optional service must state:

1. The constraint it addresses.
2. The decision authorizing it.
3. The expected behavioral or operating result.
4. The evidence used to evaluate it.

## Commercial Model

THP should not begin with an open-ended agency retainer.

The initial model should be:

## Discovery and application

Free.

Purpose:

* Educate.
* Attract.
* Route.
* Determine whether qualification review is appropriate.

## Qualification

Free during the first-client validation period.

THP should later review whether qualification consumes enough professional time to require a fee.

## Diagnostic

Paid, fixed-scope professional engagement.

The diagnostic remains valuable even when THP recommends against continuing.

## Stability Intervention

Fixed-fee, milestone-based engagement.

Recommended payment structure:

* 40% before kickoff.
* 30% after Viable Core and Constraint Diagnosis are approved.
* 30% before Level 2 Proof and final handoff.

This structure protects THP from financing the client’s intervention while connecting payment to meaningful engagement milestones.

## Optional execution services

Priced separately or explicitly included in the Scope of Work.

They should not enter the engagement informally.

## Scope changes

Any new offer, channel, campaign, branding project, website build, or operating project outside the approved constraint requires:

* A new decision.
* A scope change.
* A commercial adjustment.
* A review of whether it disrupts the intervention.

## Continuation Offers

Possible continuation offers include:

### Stability Stewardship

A limited monthly operating review for businesses that have reached or nearly reached Level 2.

It may include:

* Evidence review.
* Decision review.
* Customer and community pattern review.
* Operating-risk review.
* Level 2 consolidation.

### Level 3 Growth Experiment Support

Used only when the Level 2 foundation is confirmed.

It governs:

* Growth hypotheses.
* Experiment design.
* Protected-core boundaries.
* Cohort evidence.
* Growth-mechanism validation.

This should not be sold to a business still operating in Level 1 survival mode.

## Client Responsibilities

A client must:

* Provide accurate information.
* Provide agreed evidence.
* Participate in interviews and reviews.
* Make business decisions.
* Complete assigned founder actions.
* Communicate material changes.
* Respect operational and financial boundaries.
* Participate in customer learning.
* Avoid concealed or uncoordinated strategic changes.
* Pay according to the agreement.
* Accept that THP may produce findings they do not like.

Failure to complete these responsibilities may produce:

* Delay.
* Scope revision.
* Pause.
* Referral.
* Termination.

---

## THP Responsibilities

THP must:

* Maintain process integrity.
* Distinguish evidence from assumptions.
* Record uncertainty.
* Avoid unsupported guarantees.
* Protect client information.
* Make recommendations connected to the diagnosed constraint.
* Report contradictory evidence.
* Explain what should not be done yet.
* Maintain clear commercial boundaries.
* Record decisions.
* Complete agreed outputs.
* End or refer an engagement when continuing would be irresponsible.

---

## Completion Standard

The intervention is not complete because:

* The logo is finished.
* Content was published.
* A website was launched.
* Meetings were completed.
* Documents were delivered.
* The client feels more confident.

The intervention is complete when:

* The engagement scope has been fulfilled.
* Level 2 has been reassessed.
* The gate-by-gate evidence has been recorded.
* Remaining risks are visible.
* The client has received the systems and decisions required for continuation.
* THP has issued an honest transition conclusion.

Possible conclusions:

* Level 2 confirmed.
* Level 2 partially established.
* Continued stability work required.
* External referral required.
* Intervention paused.
* Intervention terminated.
