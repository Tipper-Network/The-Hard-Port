## Field-Validation Boundary

Working commercial defaults are recorded; final prices, contract language, and tax treatment remain pending.

## Working Commercial Defaults

> **Working recommendation — not confirmed price policy.**

- Discovery and application are free.
- Qualification is free during first-client validation.
- The diagnostic is paid and fixed-scope.
- Fifty percent of the diagnostic fee may be credited toward the intervention.
- The intervention uses milestone-based fixed fees (40% / 30% / 30% at kickoff, post-constraint approval, pre–Level 2 Proof — working default only).
- Optional execution services are explicitly included or separately priced.

## Deferred Legal and Commercial Decisions

- Final diagnostic and intervention prices
- Proposal-validity period for diagnostic credit
- Contract and scope-of-work language
- Refund, cancellation, and termination economics
- Deliverable ownership (legal language pending in research doc)
- Qualification fee after first-client validation period

THP's institutional offer:

**Level 1-to-Level 2 business stabilization.**

Branding, content, communication, customer research, community systems, and operational design.

Execution capabilities support the intervention. They do not define THP's institutional transformation.
