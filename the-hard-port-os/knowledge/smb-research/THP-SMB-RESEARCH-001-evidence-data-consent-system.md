Research, case-study, public-content, and Tipper-data permissions remain optional and separately recorded.

## 7. THP Evidence and Data System

Every engagement produces multiple categories of information. They must not be treated as one undifferentiated dataset.

## Category A — Client source data

Examples:

* Transactions.
* Customer messages.
* Financial records.
* Orders.
* Team information.
* Operational documents.
* Platform information.

Default rule:

> The client retains ownership of its source business data.

THP receives limited permission to use it for agreed service delivery.

## Category B — THP working analysis

Examples:

* Evidence inventories.
* Diagnostic notes.
* Constraint hypotheses.
* Maturity findings.
* Decision records.
* Risk registers.

These records exist to perform and defend the professional work.

## Category C — Client deliverables

Examples:

* Business maps.
* Identity foundations.
* Message systems.
* Workflows.
* Feedback systems.
* Final recommendations.

The client receives the agreed rights to use its delivered materials.

THP retains ownership of its general frameworks, methods, templates, and reusable institutional structures.

Final legal ownership language remains pending.

## Category D — Institutional learning

Examples:

* Repeated SMB patterns.
* Diagnostic failures.
* Intervention improvements.
* Maturity-model changes.
* Common constraint patterns.
* Tipper requirements.

Institutional learning should not expose identifiable client information without explicit permission.

## Category E — Anonymized research

Used only when:

* Identifying details are removed.
* Reidentification risk is reasonably controlled.
* The client has granted the relevant permission.
* The use is consistent with the stated research purpose.

## Category F — Public proof and content

Includes:

* Named case studies.
* Quotes.
* Videos.
* Screenshots.
* Financial results.
* Founder stories.
* Before-and-after material.

Public use requires separate explicit permission.

## Category G — Tipper data

Information should not be entered into Tipper merely because the business is a THP client.

Tipper use requires:

* A defined product or research purpose.
* Clear data fields.
* Appropriate client permission.
* Appropriate customer or community permissions where required.
* A justified retention and access model.

## 8. Consent Architecture

The following permissions remain separate:

## Service permission

Required to perform the engagement.

## Internal learning permission

Allows restricted internal improvement of THP’s methodology.

## Anonymized research permission

Allows non-identifying patterns to contribute to THP research.

## Case-study permission

Allows the engagement to be turned into a structured case study.

## Public-content permission

Allows names, visuals, quotes, or results to be published.

## Tipper-data permission

Allows defined information to be processed through Tipper.

A client purchasing the service does not automatically grant public, research, case-study, or Tipper permission.

## 9. Evidence Rules

THP should preserve the existing evidence statuses:

* `self_reported`
* `supported`
* `verified`
* `contradicted`
* `unavailable`
* `not_applicable`

Every major institutional conclusion should identify:

* The evidence reviewed.
* Its status.
* The interpretation.
* Alternative explanations.
* Remaining uncertainty.
* The decision produced.

THP should avoid precision theater.

A neat dashboard does not make poor evidence reliable.
