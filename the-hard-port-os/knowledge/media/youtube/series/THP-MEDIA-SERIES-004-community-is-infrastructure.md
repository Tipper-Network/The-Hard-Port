# Series — Community Is Infrastructure

> **Working promise:** Explain why a business's relationships are part of its operating infrastructure.

---

## Purpose

Institutional-thesis videos exploring customers, participation, feedback, relationships, and future Tipper research questions. Extend THP's worldview beyond individual business diagnostics.

---

## Target Viewer State

* Past early recognition; willing to think structurally about customer relationships.
* Confusing audience with community.
* Relying on reviews instead of systematic feedback.
* Interested in why THP studies business transformation as a social system.
* Not primarily seeking tactical content advice.

---

## Primary Content Category

**Institutional thesis** (Category 4)

---

## Questions This Series Answers

* What is a business as a relationship system?
* Why does community matter beyond follower count?
* Why are reviews a weak form of feedback?
* How should businesses and customers learn together?
* Why does THP study business transformation?
* What is the difference between customers and infrastructure-level relationships?

---

## Claims This Series Should Avoid

* Tipper product promises without field evidence.
* Presenting Tipper as The Hard Port or as a proven solution.
* Universal community-building prescriptions.
* Implying every microbusiness needs a community platform.
* Named client stories without permission.
* Overclaiming from manual-first research status.

---

## Appropriate Calls to Action

* Subscribe and return for the series.
* Participate in an institutional question (comment or poll).
* Follow field work—not product signup.
* Read service explanation when relationship problems connect to Level 1 survival.

Do not force Tipper promotion before evidence exists.

---

## Relationship to the First-Onboarder Model

Community and retention dimensions (C) are core Level 1 assessment areas. This series helps owners recognize that followers ≠ community—a first-onboarder distinction.

Viewer language about retention and participation feeds research and diagnostic question development.

---

## Relationship to the Intervention

Retention and community systems are intervention outputs for some entities—not universal media prescriptions.

This series frames **why** relationships matter structurally; the diagnostic determines **what** exists in a specific business.

---

## Field-Evidence Requirements

After publication, record:

* Whether viewers grasp relationship-system framing vs. audience-growth framing
* Objections to institutional thesis content
* Tipper misattribution (viewers thinking THP sells a community product)
* Connection to retention dimension language in applications
