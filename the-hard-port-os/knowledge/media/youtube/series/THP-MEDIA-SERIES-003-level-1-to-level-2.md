# Series — From Level 1 to Level 2

> **Working promise:** Show what the business must establish before increasing pressure.

---

## Purpose

Transition-led videos explaining stability gates and the path from reactive survival to deliberate stability. Introduce Level 2 conditions without delivering the full intervention.

---

## Target Viewer State

* Recognizes survival symptoms and accepts structural explanations.
* Considering growth before stability gates are met.
* Equating "Level 2" with fame, scale, or follower count.
* Willing to self-select against growth tactics that increase workload without stability.
* Approaching readiness for professional examination.

---

## Primary Content Category

**Transition** (Category 3)

Video 12 (institutional invitation) extends into application routing.

---

## Questions This Series Answers

* What must become clear before growth?
* What must become repeatable?
* What should the business protect (viable core)?
* What evidence is required?
* What should happen before attempting growth?
* What is the real Level 1 → Level 2 transition?

---

## Claims This Series Should Avoid

* That Level 2 means guaranteed growth or revenue targets.
* Specific timelines for reaching stability.
* Giving away full diagnostic or intervention procedures.
* Promising THP acceptance from video content alone.
* Growth hacks that contradict the stability doctrine.
* Implying all Level 1 businesses can reach Level 2 without examination.

---

## Appropriate Calls to Action

* **CTA Level 3 — Readiness:** Review Level 1/2 conditions, service explanation, diagnostic boundaries.
* **CTA Level 4 — Application:** Only in Video 12 when intake route is verified usable.
* Examples: "Identify which Level 2 gate is missing" · "Read `/work-with-us`" · "Complete `/apply` if fit criteria met."

---

## Relationship to the First-Onboarder Model

This series moves viewers from recognition to readiness—the stage where qualified first-onboarder applications should emerge.

Application-source attribution from transition and invitation videos is critical for measuring media fit.

---

## Relationship to the Intervention

This series describes **what the intervention addresses** without replacing it.

It references:

* Viable Core concept
* Level 2 stability gates
* Business Reality Diagnostic scope

It must not deliver diagnostic outputs, constraint maps, or intervention milestones as DIY instructions.

---

## Field-Evidence Requirements

After publication, record:

* Self-selection accuracy (do viewers identify missing gates correctly?)
* Application quality from transition-series viewers
* Misinterpretations (agency shopping, free audit requests)
* Whether `/work-with-us` visits precede applications
