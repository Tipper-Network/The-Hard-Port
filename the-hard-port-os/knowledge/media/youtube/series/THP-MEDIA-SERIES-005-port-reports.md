# Series — Port Reports

> **Working promise:** Show the institution learning in public without exposing clients or exaggerating results.

> **Series status:** `inactive` — do not produce Port Reports until real field evidence and appropriate permissions exist.

---

## Purpose

Field-learning videos documenting THP's observations, contradictions, methodology changes, and evidence from real engagements—with strict consent and anonymization boundaries.

---

## Target Viewer State

* Already trusts THP's interpretive frame.
* Wants evidence that THP builds from field work, not performance.
* Considering application or following the first field-validation cycle.
* Not seeking generic marketing advice.

---

## Primary Content Category

**Proof and field learning** (Category 5)

---

## Questions This Series Answers

* What happened with a real entity?
* Which assumptions failed?
* Which evidence was unavailable?
* What did the intervention reveal?
* What did THP change in its own methodology?
* What remains uncertain?

---

## Claims This Series Should Avoid

* Client identification without `public_content_permission`.
* Financial results without context and permission.
* Diagnostic conclusions presented as universal laws.
* Success stories that omit starting condition and limitations.
* Before/after claims that exaggerate intervention effect.
* Anonymous patterns that enable reasonable client reidentification.

---

## Appropriate Calls to Action

* **CTA Level 5 — Field participation:** Follow the field cycle, submit relevant observation, apply for later validation cycle.
* Application CTA only when aligned with explicit invitation content and intake readiness.

---

## Relationship to the First-Onboarder Model

Port Reports validate—or falsify—the first-onboarder model using real entity evidence.

They must distinguish institutional learning from marketing proof.

---

## Relationship to the Intervention

Port Reports document intervention **learning**, not intervention **delivery**.

They may reference:

* Starting maturity classification
* Methodology changes
* Failed assumptions
* Evidence gaps

They must not substitute for client deliverables or case-study products without separate permission.

---

## Field-Evidence Requirements

Before any Port Report is published:

1. Real engagement or field-validation cycle completed or materially underway
2. Permission level recorded per [`THP-SMB-PROOF-001`](../../smb-proof/THP-SMB-PROOF-001-case-study-and-proof-system.md)
3. Consent architecture satisfied per [`THP-SMB-RESEARCH-001`](../../smb-research/THP-SMB-RESEARCH-001-evidence-data-consent-system.md)
4. THP institutional learning record updated
5. Legal review for public content if named or identifiable material included

## Activation Condition

Activate this series when:

* At least one first-entity engagement produces documentable institutional learning
* Appropriate proof permission is recorded
* Anonymization or public-content consent is explicit
* THP can publish without exposing clients or exaggerating results

Until then, keep `series_status: inactive`.
