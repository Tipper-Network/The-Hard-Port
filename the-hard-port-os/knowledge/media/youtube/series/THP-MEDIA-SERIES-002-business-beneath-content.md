# Series — The Business Beneath the Content

> **Working promise:** Stop treating the social account as though it is the entire business.

---

## Purpose

Interpretation-led videos examining what content performance can and cannot reveal about an underlying business. Replace algorithm-blame and content-fix thinking with structural explanations.

---

## Target Viewer State

* Blaming content quality, posting frequency, or the algorithm for unstable sales.
* Conflating followers, engagement, and customers.
* Investing in branding while core demand and delivery remain unclear.
* Measuring visible activity instead of business effects.
* Ready to examine assumptions after recognizing survival symptoms.

---

## Primary Content Category

**Interpretation** (Category 2)

---

## Questions This Series Answers

* Is the problem really the algorithm?
* Is attention producing demand?
* Does the business understand why customers buy?
* Are followers, customers, and community being confused?
* Is branding being used to avoid a business question?
* What does the social account reveal—and hide—about the business underneath?

---

## Claims This Series Should Avoid

* That content never matters (content is a signal surface).
* That THP is anti-marketing or anti-social-media.
* Specific algorithm advice or platform tactics.
* Claiming to know what's wrong with a viewer's business without examination.
* Positioning THP as a posting or content agency.
* Guaranteed lead or conversion promises.

---

## Appropriate Calls to Action

* **CTA Level 2 — Evidence:** Ask the viewer to observe customer behavior or verify an assumption.
* Examples: "Compare engagement with purchases" · "Map one customer cycle" · "Separate brand claims from customer experience."
* Link to `/work-with-us` in description when appropriate; application CTA only in later sequence videos.

---

## Relationship to the First-Onboarder Model

This series filters viewers who conflate content metrics with business health— a common first-onboarder misinterpretation.

Evidence CTAs test whether viewers will perform observation, a founder-readiness signal.

---

## Relationship to the Intervention

This series builds **interpretive authority** without delivering diagnostic outputs.

It introduces distinctions (attention/demand, followers/customers/community, branding/identity) that the paid diagnostic investigates with evidence.

Must not present THP diagnostic findings as if already completed for the viewer.

---

## Field-Evidence Requirements

After publication, record:

* Which distinctions produced strongest agreement or disagreement
* Whether viewers performed requested observations (comment evidence)
* Misinterpretations (e.g., "THP says stop posting")
* Application quality from viewers who entered after interpretation videos
