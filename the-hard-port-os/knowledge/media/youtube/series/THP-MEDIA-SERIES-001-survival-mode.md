# Series — Survival Mode

> **Working promise:** Name the condition the owner has been living inside but could not clearly describe.

---

## Purpose

Recognition-led videos exposing the daily and structural reality of Level 1 survival. Help established microbusiness owners see that their experience has a name, a pattern, and a cause deeper than "bad marketing."

---

## Target Viewer State

* Posting, promoting, or rebranding without business stability improving.
* Feeling busy but unable to predict revenue.
* Depending on rescue events (viral posts, large clients, seasonal spikes).
* Beginning to wonder whether the business itself—not the algorithm—is the problem.
* Willing to recognize symptoms before seeking solutions.

---

## Primary Content Category

**Recognition** (Category 1)

May blend into transition when naming survival mode structurally (see Video 9).

---

## Questions This Series Answers

* What does survival mode look like day to day?
* Why does the business feel active but remain unstable?
* Why do repeated marketing activities stop producing clarity?
* Why does the founder feel personally trapped inside the business?
* What is the difference between being busy and making progress?

---

## Claims This Series Should Avoid

* That the owner is lazy, stupid, or incompetent.
* That THP guarantees transformation or growth.
* That one tactic (posting, boosting, rebranding) always causes survival mode.
* Universal prescriptions without evidence boundaries.
* Diagnostic conclusions about specific unaudited businesses.
* Promising that recognition alone fixes the business.

---

## Appropriate Calls to Action

* **CTA Level 1 — Recognition:** Comment, self-observation, return for next video.
* Examples: "What have you already tried?" · "Which part describes your business?" · "List what changed after the last month of posting."
* Do not push application in early Survival Mode videos.

---

## Relationship to the First-Onboarder Model

This series attracts and filters the first-onboarder profile: established Level 1 microbusiness owners who recognize survival patterns in themselves.

Recognition comments and viewer language from this series feed the first-onboarder model definition and application-source attribution.

---

## Relationship to the Intervention

Survival Mode videos **name the condition**; they do not deliver the intervention.

They must not give away:

* Full diagnostic methodology
* Constraint diagnosis procedures
* Viable Core identification process
* Level 2 gate implementation steps

They should point toward interpretation and transition series—not substitute for paid diagnostic work.

---

## Field-Evidence Requirements

After publication, record per video:

* Recognition comments from real business owners
* Objections and misinterpretations
* Viewer language matching Level 1 dimensions
* Whether CTA produced intended self-observation
