# THP YouTube Media System

> **Status:** `working` · **Not canonical.**  
> First 12-video field test pending.

## Institutional Position

YouTube is the public attraction and interpretation layer of THP.

It does not replace qualification, diagnosis, intervention, or proof.

Its purpose is to attract the correct Level 1 owner and route suitable viewers
toward the Engagement Readiness Application.

**Production source of truth for the first twelve videos:** [`content/youtube/first-50-observational-videos/00-FIRST-50-FILMING-MAP.md`](../../content/youtube/first-50-observational-videos/00-FIRST-50-FILMING-MAP.md) — **canonical entry point** (FND-001–012 · OBS-001–038).

## Channel Purpose

The Hard Port uses YouTube to:

1. Make survival-mode business conditions visible.
2. Challenge false explanations for those conditions.
3. Give owners a more accurate way to understand their businesses.
4. Explain the transition from Level 1 survival to Level 2 stability.
5. Attract businesses ready for examination, not merely content production.
6. Generate language, objections, and evidence that improve THP.
7. Route suitable owners toward the Engagement Readiness Application.

---

## Channel Thesis

> The Hard Port investigates why established microbusinesses remain trapped in survival mode and builds the systems that help them become stable enough to grow responsibly.

**Public-facing version:** Your business may not have a content problem. It may have a clarity, evidence, relationship, or operating problem that content cannot solve.

**Channel promise:** We will help you see the business underneath the posts.

Full architecture: [`THP-MEDIA-001-youtube-attraction-architecture.md`](./THP-MEDIA-001-youtube-attraction-architecture.md)

---

## Target Viewer

The founder of an established microbusiness who:

* Already sells to real customers.
* Operates online or depends materially on online communication.
* Remains personally involved in most business activity.
* Experiences inconsistent sales, unclear demand, weak retention, operational pressure, or identity confusion.
* Has already tried content, trends, boosting, branding, promotions, or hiring.
* Is beginning to suspect the problem is deeper than visibility.
* Is trying to move from survival toward stability.
* Is willing to examine the business rather than only blame the algorithm.

Level 1 definition: [`THP-SMB-LEVEL-01`](../../smb-maturity/levels/THP-SMB-LEVEL-01-established-but-surviving.md)

---

## Non-Target Viewers

The channel is not primarily built for:

* Pre-launch founders with no paying customers.
* General entrepreneurship motivation seekers.
* Creators seeking only audience-growth tactics.
* Established companies already preparing to scale.
* Businesses seeking guaranteed leads or virality.
* Founders unwilling to examine customer evidence.
* Marketing professionals looking only for content tips.
* Viewers seeking a done-for-you posting service without business investigation.
