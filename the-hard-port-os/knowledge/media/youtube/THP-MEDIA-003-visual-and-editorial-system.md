# THP Visual and Editorial System

> **Status:** `working` · **Not canonical.**

Kinetic typography should emphasize conceptual distinctions rather than
display every spoken word.

The visual system should make hidden business relationships visible.

---

## Grid and Systems Language

THP YouTube content should develop a recognizable systems-oriented visual language:

* **Grids** — structure, comparison, and layered information
* **Maps** — customer journeys, business cycles, relationship topology
* **Coordinates** — position within maturity or stability space
* **Gates** — Level 2 stability conditions and transition thresholds
* **Loops** — feedback cycles, retention patterns, institutional learning
* **Layers** — surface (content) versus underlying (business system)
* **Evidence labels** — distinguish observation, assumption, verified, and unavailable
* **Business-cycle diagrams** — discovery → purchase → delivery → response
* **Signal-versus-noise contrasts** — engagement metrics versus business effects
* **Movement from instability toward structure** — visual narrative of Level 1 → Level 2

---

## Maps, Coordinates, Gates, Loops, and Evidence Labels

| Element | Use |
|---|---|
| **Maps** | Show where the viewer's business sits relative to stability gates |
| **Coordinates** | Plot dimensions (demand, retention, operations) without fake precision |
| **Gates** | Mark Level 2 conditions that must exist before growth |
| **Loops** | Show how customer behavior feeds—or fails to feed—business learning |
| **Evidence labels** | Tag claims as `self_reported`, `supported`, `verified`, or `unavailable` |

Do not use dashboards or charts that imply precision the evidence does not support.

---

## Kinetic Typography Purpose

Kinetic typography should emphasize **conceptual distinctions** rather than display every spoken word.

Words worth emphasizing:

* Attention
* Demand
* Evidence
* Customer
* Community
* Constraint
* Stability
* Growth
* System
* Reality

Use kinetic type at moment of distinction—when the video separates two concepts the viewer has been conflating.

---

## Founder Narration

Format D (Founder Reality) uses direct narration.

Tone requirements:

* Direct
* Investigative
* Calm
* Occasionally sarcastic
* Unimpressed by marketing clichés
* Respectful of the founder without flattering them
* Honest about uncertainty

Avoid pity, hustle culture, empty reassurance, and motivational speeches.

---

## Diagram Use

Diagrams should:

* Make hidden business relationships visible
* Separate surface metrics from underlying systems
* Show customer cycles, not content funnels alone
* Label uncertainty where interpretation exceeds evidence

Diagrams should not:

* Imply diagnostic conclusions about unaudited businesses
* Use fake dashboard aesthetics
* Present universal prescriptions as verified

---

## Port Metaphor

The port is a place of:

* **Arrival** — the viewer recognizes their condition
* **Inspection** — THP examines evidence, not ego
* **Organization** — structure replaces reactive survival
* **Departure** — the owner leaves with clarity, not empty motivation

Visual language may reference nautical and port imagery consistent with THP brand—but as systems metaphor, not decorative wallpaper.

---

## Tone

Editorial tone across all media:

* Confrontational without humiliation
* Structural over tactical
* Evidence-seeking over certainty-performing
* Boundaried about what THP can and cannot claim

Copy should pass the voice litmus: care about the work and outcome; do not care about the viewer's comfort.

---

## Editorial Boundaries

THP media should not:

* Publish client details without permission
* Present financial claims without context
* Diagnose businesses that were not examined
* Offer generic prescriptive advice as universal truth
* Promote Tipper as a proven product before field evidence exists
* Publish "how to grow quickly" content that contradicts the stability doctrine
* Chase trends unrelated to the first-onboarder model
* Humiliate struggling owners
* Perform fake certainty

Content should challenge the owner without turning the owner into the problem.

---

## Prohibited Visual Clichés

Do not use:

* Generic entrepreneur stock footage
* Luxury-business imagery
* Hustle culture visuals
* Empty motivational montages
* Fake dashboard or analytics visuals
* Unverified success statistics on screen
* Excessive algorithm-blame framing
* Advertisement-style every-video sell

---

## Client-Evidence Permission Requirements

Before using any client-derived visual or narrative evidence:

1. Confirm the relevant permission level per [`THP-SMB-PROOF-001`](../smb-proof/THP-SMB-PROOF-001-case-study-and-proof-system.md)
2. Record consent status in the proof or engagement record
3. Anonymize where `anonymous_evidence_note` is the maximum permission
4. Do not enable reasonable client reidentification from anonymous patterns

Media usefulness does not override client consent.

Port Reports and proof-category content remain inactive until permissions exist.
