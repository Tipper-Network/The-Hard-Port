# THP First 12-Video Test

> **Status:** `working` · All videos `not_started`.  
> One deliberate sequence — not a content calendar.

## Act I — Recognition

---

### Video 1

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Video number** | 1 |
| **Working title** | Posting Consistently Is Not a Business Strategy |
| **Category** | Recognition |
| **Series** | Survival Mode |
| **Target viewer condition** | Level 1 owner posting consistently without observable business learning |
| **Existing belief** | More consistent posting will eventually fix the business |
| **Central claim** | Repetition without business learning only makes the same uncertainty more expensive |
| **Intended reframe** | Activity is not learning; posting without evidence review is expensive repetition |
| **Viewer observation** | List what changed in the business after the last month of posting |
| **Call to action** | Recognition — comment or write down what changed after the last month of posting |
| **Level 1 / Level 2 dimensions** | G (Evidence and decision-making), A (Demand) |
| **Production status** | `not_started` |
| **Learning question** | Do owners recognize that posting activity does not equal business progress? |

## Act II — Interpretation

---

### Video 5

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Video number** | 5 |
| **Working title** | Followers, Customers, and Community Are Not the Same |
| **Category** | Interpretation |
| **Series** | The Business Beneath the Content |
| **Target viewer condition** | Level 1 owner treating followers, buyers, and repeat customers as one audience |
| **Existing belief** | A larger audience will eventually become a stable customer base |
| **Central claim** | These groups display different behaviors and should not be measured as one audience |
| **Intended reframe** | Observers, buyers, repeat customers, referrers, and participants are distinct relationship types |
| **Viewer observation** | Sort known people into observers, buyers, repeat customers, referrers, and participants |
| **Call to action** | Evidence — categorize at least ten known people by relationship type |
| **Level 1 / Level 2 dimensions** | B (Customer understanding), C (Retention and community) |
| **Production status** | `not_started` |
| **Learning question** | Do owners discover they have observers but not a community? |

## Act III — Transition and Invitation

---

### Video 9

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Video number** | 9 |
| **Working title** | What Survival Mode Actually Means |
| **Category** | Recognition and transition |
| **Series** | Survival Mode · From Level 1 to Level 2 |
| **Target viewer condition** | Level 1 owner who recognizes symptoms but lacks a name for the condition |
| **Existing belief** | Low revenue or slow months define the problem |
| **Central claim** | Survival mode is not merely low revenue; it is a condition where immediate pressure controls decisions and the business cannot reliably interpret itself |
| **Intended reframe** | Survival is a systemic condition across demand, evidence, operations, and founder dependency—not a revenue number |
| **Viewer observation** | Review the eight Level 1 dimensions (A–H) |
| **Call to action** | Readiness — identify which Level 1 dimensions feel most accurate |
| **Level 1 / Level 2 dimensions** | All Level 1 dimensions A–H |
| **Production status** | `not_started` |
| **Learning question** | Does naming survival mode increase self-selection accuracy in comments? |

|---|---|

| **Working title** | We Are Looking for the First THP Entity |
| **Category** | Institutional invitation |
| **Series** | From Level 1 to Level 2 |
| **Target viewer condition** | Level 1 owner ready for evidence-based examination who matches first-onboarder profile |
| **Existing belief** | THP is another marketing service or free audit |
| **Central claim** | THP is beginning its first field-validation engagement with one established online or online-dependent microbusiness |
| **Intended reframe** | THP offers a paid, bounded diagnostic—not content production or growth guarantees |
| **Viewer observation** | Complete the Engagement Readiness Application |
| **Call to action** | Application — complete `/apply` if fit criteria are met |
| **Level 1 / Level 2 dimensions** | First-onboarder fit; all qualification dimensions |
| **Production status** | `not_started` |
| **Learning question** | Does Video 12 produce qualified applications from Level 1 owners—not pre-launch founders or agency shoppers? |

**Publication gate:** Do not publish Video 12 with a direct application CTA until the intake readiness gate in [`THP-FIRST-CLIENT-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md`](../../operations/first-client-readiness/THP-FIRST-CLIENT-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md) is satisfied.

The invitation must state:

* Who qualifies.
* Who does not.
* What evidence is required.
* That the diagnostic is paid.
* That acceptance is limited.
* That THP does not guarantee growth.
* What THP intends to learn.
* What information will remain private.
