# THP SMB Intervention System

## Purpose

Define how THP moves an accepted Level 1 business toward the Level 2 operating foundation.

## Intervention Position

The intervention begins after:

1. Engagement Readiness Application
2. First-Onboarder Qualification
3. Initial Level 1 Diagnostic
4. Findings presentation
5. Founder agreement to proceed

It ends with Level 2 reassessment, continuation, referral, pause, or termination.

Full engagement sequence:

> Application → Qualification → Diagnostic → Findings → Intervention → Reassessment

## Official Phase Order

1. Business Reality
2. Evidence Recovery
3. Viable Core
4. Constraint Diagnosis
5. Identity Clarification
6. Relationship Foundation
7. Operating Foundation
8. Level 2 Proof

Evidence Recovery precedes Viable Core so the viable core is identified from recovered business evidence, not founder recollection alone.

> **Historical note:** The 0.1.0 intervention shell (2026-07-21) listed Viable Core before Evidence Recovery as a provisional order. That sequence is preserved in revision history only.

## Execution and Content Boundaries

Branding, content, communication, customer research, workflow design, and other execution services are instruments used inside the intervention.

They are not the institutional transformation by themselves.

Every execution deliverable must identify:

- The diagnosed constraint
- The authorizing decision
- The intended outcome
- The evidence used for review
