# Archive

Retired, superseded, or raw source material retained for provenance. Archive documents are not canonical and should not be used as current operating instructions.

## What Belongs Here

Obsolete, superseded, session-specific, or raw provenance material that must remain addressable.

Completed session task briefs from `../active/CURRENT_TASK.md` belong under `session-XX/` after the work finishes.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Current research, active RFCs, accepted decisions, current system specifications, SOPs, or Canon chapters.

## How Documents Enter

Material enters after a replacement, supersession, or migration has preserved links and history.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

Archive records remain. They may be referenced for provenance but do not regain operational authority without a new decision and working-layer update.
