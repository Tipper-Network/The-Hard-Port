# Research Program 001

## Research Object

How conscious beings construct, inhabit, transcend, and reconstruct the systems that define their reality.

## Hypothesis H-001

In the absence of conscious self-authorship, human behavior becomes increasingly shaped by external systems, identities, incentives, and expectations.

## Research Environment

Businesses are the primary research laboratory.

Consulting creates proximity.
Media creates attraction.
Tipper captures living behavioral data.
Research improves consulting.
Consulting improves businesses.
Businesses generate new evidence.
Tipper scales validated knowledge.
