
## New principles emerging from this section

These should remain working principles until tested, rather than immediately being declared canonical.

### Principle 1 — Definitions are instruments

> A definition should organize attention and action without being mistaken for the complete reality it describes.

### Principle 2 — Competence must evolve

> Competence is relative to present conditions; methods that produced success may create dependency when those conditions change.

### Principle 3 — Perceptual debt compounds

> The longer an operative model remains detached from current reality, the more disruptive its eventual correction becomes.

### Principle 4 — Attention sustains contact with reality

> Attention is the continuous reconciliation of an internal model with the changing shared world.

### Principle 5 — Invisible systems require perceptible models

> People struggle to construct what they cannot perceive; conceptual clarity allows missing organizational systems to exert a pull toward action.

### Principle 6 — Building continues after opening

> The visible business can be complete while the organization required to sustain it remains unfinished.

### Principle 7 — Continuity requires transformation

> A system preserves itself not through fixed form, but through its capacity to alter itself while retaining relational coherence.
