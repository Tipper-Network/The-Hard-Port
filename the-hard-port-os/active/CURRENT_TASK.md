# Current Task

Build the Level 1 Diagnostic Interview Across Dimensions A–H.

## Purpose

Determine the business's actual Level 1 conditions, identify which Level 2 gates are already present or absent, locate the primary constraint, and recommend the first THP intervention.

## Dimensions

A. Demand  
B. Customer Understanding  
C. Retention and Community  
D. Financial Stability  
E. Operations  
F. Founder Dependency  
G. Evidence and Decision-Making  
H. Identity and Market Position

## Required Outputs

- Diagnostic questions for each dimension
- Evidence required for each finding
- Supported, contradicted, and uncertain findings
- Level 1 condition assessment
- Level 2 gap assessment
- Primary and secondary constraints
- Immediate risks
- Recommended first intervention
- What THP should not do yet

- Full intervention curriculum
- YouTube architecture
- Levels 4–7
