# SOPs

## What Belongs Here

Repeatable procedures that execute an accepted system specification.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Research methods under investigation, undecided process proposals, decision rationale, system architecture, or Canon synthesis.

## How Documents Enter

An SOP enters when its governing system, owner, trigger, inputs, steps, outputs, controls, and review conditions are defined.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

An SOP is revised or superseded when the governing system or accepted decision changes. Old versions remain linked for operational history.

## Related Folders

- `../systems/` defines what operates.
- `../decisions/` explains accepted institutional choices.
- `../adrs/` explains technical choices.
- `../canon/` may synthesize stable operating principles without copying procedures.
- `../archive/` preserves retired procedures.
