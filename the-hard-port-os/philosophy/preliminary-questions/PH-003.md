1. What is a value?

ANSWER: a value is a core fundemental unshakeable belief that an entity has. be it a human or company. a value dictates the kind of entity it is, is it honest, blunt, funky, serious...
the values are what the work is built on

ANSWER: a principle is how the entities operate with the values at teh core. how everything is done in th eentity's settings
" operate as if the customer is your family member" (value is falmiliarity.) a principle is the technique of approach


3. What is the difference between valuing something and consistently
   acting according to it?

ANSWER: valuing something consistently is measuring it. consistiently acting according to it means every step you take you are making sure you are in harmony or getting better at harmonising with it. 
its the build up of skills in a certain style of approach


4. Can a person possess values they have never consciously chosen?

ANSWER: yes, some people refuse to harm animals without ever decidiing it to be the case. 
I for example always chose not to work with people that dont know why the yare working with something ( people who didnt have intent) until i sat with my self and discussed the behavior and understaood the values while building the principles around it.

Where do those values come from?

nurture and nature. 
"some we learn from our surroundings, and some are just a part of our psychological being." 


5. Can two values conflict?

ANSWER: yes, they might conflict but tha just means there is not yet harmony in the principles that guide them.

How should a person decide which value takes priority?

i would want to say the situation always take priority. but the relatinsihip they have with themselves will reflect on the stronger value.

6. Can a principle be wrong, harmful, or based on false information?

ANSWER: everything is iterable and subject to change. but if it changes too much, then there is a heavy chance it was never true to begin with.

What should cause someone to revise a principle?

values and principles are the base to the vision and mission one has. 
they are from the inside out through the self ( the values and missions) while the vison and mission are what we are connecting with on the outside( the expression of our reality into the real world.)

any time there is a misalignmnet between the inside world and the expression from it to the outside world should point at something to be revised.

   [ ] Discovers
   [ ] Chooses
   [ ] Inherits
   [ ] Develops
   [ ] Demonstrates through action
   [x] All or several of these

ANSWER: some values you discover, while others you choose ( probably cultural things) you can even decidde to take avalue from someone you love, respect. or develop a value that harmonises you and the environment.
but iwthout hte principles demonstrating the values you have through action, its just a false character that you speak about. and even when people listen. the world only understands action as proof of who you are.


8. What does “will” mean without using the words spirit, energy, intention,
   motivation, or desire?

ANSWER: will means drive, a pull of the attention in a certain direction. 


9. Can a person possess a clear intention but lack the will to act?

ANSWER: of course, the relationship between the person and their mind is different than the relationship between them and their heart.

What develops will?

 feelings that remove any friction from the vision one has

10. Can will become harmful when it refuses to respond to consequences?

ANSWER: every relationship that is not revisited, will result in a form of misalignment that could become harmful be it to the individual, the entity or the community surrounding it 

What distinguishes resolve from stubbornness?

think stubbornness: (emotional ), opinionated/judgy ( mental) and resolve ( the meeting of both which comes with a destructuring of feeling and concept and re structuring of the next steps neccessary)

11. Can an entity possess values and principles independently of the people
    currently controlling it?

ANSWER: yes, i feel that is what people call professionalism. althougg i feel company culture would create that in big companies and in small businesses the connection between teh owners and the people. 
but if the values of the individual do not complement the entity then the relationship will never work and the entity will never be able to explore its true potential. 


How are those values preserved when leadership changes?

leadership should be chosen based on the harmony of those values or the company will shift to a new approach with new values or just collapse from the lack of coherence 

12. When an entity’s stated values conflict with its repeated actions,
    which should THP treat as the stronger evidence of its governing values?

ANSWER: the repeated actions taken are more of evidence than the stated .
 but THP will also judge teh actions and the energy, time, effort... they consume. 
sometimes the actions are repeated becasue they are the only things known. maybe other approaches through new info and education realignes with the values 

13. Should THP help people choose values or only help them examine the
    values already influencing them?

ANSWER: of course. everythning in between their current position to the desired one needs to be assessed.


14. What authority should THP have to challenge a person’s or entity’s values?

ANSWER: THP has the authority of the mediator between their current position and the desired outcome. we negotiate their future. so the more authority they give us to ask, check question then the better the results of THPs existence for them is. 


15. Complete these statements:

ANSWER: if the values themselves conflict then the operations are not ethical. THP will not work with such entities ( as they would conflict with THP's values and principles.)  
just as they have the right to choose us, we need to remember that we have the same right choosing who to work with.
