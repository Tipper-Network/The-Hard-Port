# The Hard Port Charter

## Status and Scope

This document preserves The Hard Port's broader institutional thesis.

It is an exploratory philosophy, not a replacement for the canonical mission in [`intelligence/layer-00-knowledge-engine.md`](../intelligence/layer-00-knowledge-engine.md):

> Understand how small businesses work.

Candidate foundational philosophy is consolidated in [`docs/00-foundations/THP-FOUNDATIONAL-PHILOSOPHY.md`](../docs/00-foundations/THP-FOUNDATIONAL-PHILOSOPHY.md). Candidate institutional purpose and boundaries are in [`docs/00-foundations/THP-INSTITUTIONAL-PURPOSE-AND-BOUNDARIES.md`](../docs/00-foundations/THP-INSTITUTIONAL-PURPOSE-AND-BOUNDARIES.md). This charter preserves Session 01 source material and does not supersede those documents without an explicit decision.

Businesses are the first research laboratory. Any expansion of the canonical mission beyond small businesses requires an RFC and an explicit versioned decision.

## Why The Hard Port Exists

Human beings naturally become identified with the systems they inhabit. As identification increases, observation may decrease. When observation decreases, intentional redesign becomes more difficult.

The Hard Port exists to restore the distance required for clear observation, helping people redesign the systems they participate in rather than becoming unconsciously defined by them.

Transformation is not the destination. Transformation creates the conditions under which meaningful research, intentional design, and conscious participation become possible.

## Core Research Direction

The Hard Port is an institutional research and development organization.

It sustains itself commercially while investigating:

> How do conscious beings construct, inhabit, transcend, and reconstruct the systems that shape their reality?

Businesses are the first laboratory.

Communities may become a second.

Technology is one medium through which supported knowledge may be operationalized and tested at greater scale.

## Foundational Beliefs

These are working propositions, not established facts:

- Businesses are systems.
- Communities are systems.
- Identities are adaptive.
- Humans may identify with systems they repeatedly inhabit.
- Clear observation may require psychological or structural distance.
- Better models of reality can produce better decisions.
- Knowledge must remain revisable through evidence.

## Identity Transformation Cycle

```text
Old Identity
  ↓
Identification
  ↓
Shell Fracture
  ↓
Vulnerability
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
New Perception
  ↓
First Principles
  ↓
Intentional Design
  ↓
Adaptive Identity
  ↓
Reality Changes
  ↓
Repeat
```

This is a proposed human identity model. It is distinct from the business capability transformation defined in [`intelligence/layer-01-client-transformation-blueprint.md`](../intelligence/layer-01-client-transformation-blueprint.md).

The objective is not endless destruction of identity. The objective is maintaining the capacity to revise one's model of reality.

## Research Requirements

Before this philosophy can influence canonical Intelligence, it needs:

- Operational definitions for identification, observation, and self-authorship
- Supporting and contradicting observations
- Boundary conditions
- A distinction between individual, organizational, and community behavior
- Explicit risks created by applying an identity model to client businesses
- Evidence that the model improves observation, diagnosis, or intervention
