# Philosophy

Exploratory institutional beliefs, first principles, and questions that may inform research and Canon review.

Philosophy may challenge or inspire the working system, but it does not override `../systems/`, `../decisions/`, or `../canon/`. Changes to the mission, ontology, methodology, or Tipper framework must pass through research and RFC review.

## What Belongs Here

Exploratory worldview, first principles, conceptual models, and foundational questions.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Accepted operational specifications, procedures, raw evidence, positioning copy, or claims presented as canonical.

## How Documents Enter

Philosophical material enters as explicitly non-canonical or candidate work with provenance, scope, and unresolved questions.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

Supported material may inform research, RFCs, and Canon synthesis. Replaced documents retain supersession links or move to `../archive/`.
