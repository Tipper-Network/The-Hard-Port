FORM ID: THP-FQ-001
TOPIC: The origin of The Hard Port
STATUS: Founder response

1. Before you had the name “The Hard Port,” what problem, frustration,
   observation, or question kept returning to you?

ANSWER:the frustration was a disconnect with what i was trying to do and the people connecting with it. i found that ever step i took regarding the business i had was limited to how i saw things, and the extention of what i was tyringt to create was not delivered to the people i was attempting to connect with. wasted efforts, wasted money so i felt good for a short time then dont see a change in sales. i literally felt like i was attacking a wild elk with a sling shot so i dont die of hunger.
i was craved to build on what i did not repeat the same day as yesterday. 
i searched daily ffor action elements to  do even at the cost of my own friendships and health under the guize that i was at least putting effort into the business and not feel guilty that it was me that is making it fail.


2. Describe one real moment, period, experience, or relationship that made this problem feel real rather than theoretical.

ANSWER: met with web developers, set up a website, decided on building an instagram structured account, spent 1000 a month for 3 months of money i didnt have only to at one point to realize that i was just doing these things to keep busy, the goal that would have translated things to the business were not really there/ the core of my decisioni taking/ choices i made were personal not financial or development... 


3. What did you believe people, businesses, communities, or society were
   failing to see?

ANSWER: they were failing to see that who they are has nothing to do with what others are. and since we are not able to see that we are separate from ourselves . "me is from the outside in, myself is from the inside out " then we were failing to see things separate than the identity we move with. 


4. What potential did you believe was being wasted?

ANSWER: i felt up to 97% of energy spent was wasted. i felt that all we had to do is have 1% more clarity and we could save 50% of our time and energy. if we just had the clear narration, understanding, structure , format. then we wont havet o be wasting anything ourside of the desired moves we wish to make.


5. Who or what did you initially blame for this problem?

ANSWER: i blamed society for objectifiying the world, for being developed in a consumer like fasion. everything from banking to agriculture, art and even education has become consumerised. and with the late rise of ecommerce, the internet has just become a place for people to be blasted with ads and short form content, shaving off the awaremess, attention and focus that is neccesary for humans to get out of any situation


6. Has your understanding of the cause changed? What do you believe now?

ANSWER: i see how we as humans bave been complicit in this new system, and that more than ever now we are motivated to try to do things differently. the sad part is we are doing things from a position of incompetence, lack of clarity and unfocused energy that we already were failures in. kinda like treating a broken bone with a rock massage.


7. Why was creating a company, product, media channel, or community alone not enough? Why did this need to become something like The Hard Port?

ANSWER: one day i realized that i waas looking at the world from inside the system it has created. so i took my time describing the system, explaining it , understanding it, while thinking of a system that will be more subjective and has value translated in the form of a barter ( people decide the value based on their subjective views and needs. not just money, cash, products) in a world where services will become the only thing connecting it together. ( objective consumer based businesses are allready well designed) 
it is then where Tipper started being developed as a separate system to connect to the economy. there is a lot of details around how people will interact differently, businesses benefit. but the deeper i dovethe more i saw how there was the missing link between the layman and Tipper 
there is where THP came to be. we provide the service that nobody wants to provide ( strictly because it takes more effort and working with people is not fun, its much easier to just give them a product and get paid for it ( commerce...)) so THP will basically be the transitional path to the Tipper system/network.


8. Complete this sentence without polishing it:

   “The Hard Port had to exist because ________________________________.”

ANSWER: the new world requires a clarity that died with the attention it destroyed. it requires a separation of the identities between the internet, house, work, society...


9. What part of this origin are you least certain about today?

ANSWER: i cant find any certainty anywhere. it feels like everytime i talk about it more of the details i have discovered, researched and dug into fill in gaps i didnt know existed


10. What important part of the origin did these questions fail to ask about?

ANSWER: they failed to ask about how the truth behind human connection is what formed this owrld, and how the hard port will shift the direction this world is diving into.
