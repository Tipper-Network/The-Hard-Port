# Open Philosophical Questions

> **Purpose:** Consolidated registry of unresolved philosophical questions.  
> **Policy:** THP-PH-001 through THP-PH-004 are [frozen candidate working philosophy](./CANDIDATE-WORKING-PHILOSOPHY.md). Do not add new philosophy forms to `active/CURRENT_TASK.md`. Let operating evidence sharpen answers.

Questions here may be advanced through research, SMB work, and bounded experiments — not through additional preliminary philosophy forms unless explicitly reopened by decision.

## Priority Themes

These themes remain explicitly open across the frozen candidate positions:

### Organizational consciousness

- Can an organization possess awareness, or only identity and agency? (THP-PH-002)
- What evidence could support or falsify organizational consciousness? (THP-PH-002)
- Does organizational behavior ever exceed the separate intentions of individual members? (THP-PH-002)
- Whether the term *consciousness* provides explanatory value beyond identity, agency, culture, and governance. (THP-PH-002)

**Operational term until resolved:** organizational identity.  
**Research hypothesis:** [`HYP-002-organizational-consciousness.md`](../../research/hypotheses/HYP-002-organizational-consciousness.md) — exploratory only; not fact, Canon, or confirmed Tipper property.

### Spirit and consciousness

- Is consciousness produced by physical processes, fundamental to reality, or something else? (THP-PH-001)
- Does *spirit* identify a distinct phenomenon, or does it combine will, meaning, motivation, and consciousness? (THP-PH-001)
- Consciousness as pure energy — founder language in PH-001; metaphysical nature unresolved
- All existence created by consciousness ("everything is mind") — founder language; not THP's institutional position on external existence
- Spirit as metaphysical force — provisional operational language only in THP-PH-001 §12; ultimate nature unresolved

### Degrees of agency

- Can agency exist in degrees, and what is the minimum necessary for agency to be meaningful rather than merely theoretical? (PH-004 testimony; institutional synthesis deferred)
- How should THP distinguish lack of will from lack of capability or opportunity? (THP-PH-003)
- When does a system reduce effort while increasing agency — or demand effort while reducing agency? (PH-004 testimony)
- Whether organizations, communities, or non-human agents count as conscious beings with agency for specific THP purposes (THP-PHILOSOPHY-001)

### Personal continuity

- What level of continuity is necessary for personal responsibility across major personal change? (THP-PH-002)
- What precisely constitutes the self beyond the provisional model? (THP-PH-001 §12, THP-PHILOSOPHY-001)
- How should unconscious and habitual processes be represented in the model of the self? (THP-PH-002)
- How should THP distinguish genuine intention from retrospective justification? (THP-PH-002)

### Moral responsibility under severe constraint

- How should values be prioritized when every available decision causes meaningful harm? (THP-PH-003)
- Can someone cause an outcome without moral responsibility when control, foresight, coercion, information, or capacity are limited? (PH-004 testimony; THP-PH-003 §15)
- What limits the responsibility a person may legitimately assume for an outcome they did not cause? (PH-004 testimony — competence, authority, capacity, consent, proportionality, existing duties)
- Can responsibility always be claimed without becoming misplaced blame? (THP-PHILOSOPHY-001, THP-PH-001 §11)
- How should voluntary sacrifice be distinguished from normalized or misunderstood harm? (THP-PH-001)
- How should legitimacy be evaluated when full consent is impossible? (THP-PH-001)

### Limits of institutional accountability

- Which consequences should an institution be accountable for — directly caused, materially contributed, foreseeable, preventable, continued-after-notice, or broader? (PH-004 testimony; THP-PH-003 §14–§16)
- What limits THP's continuing obligation when client harm is suspected? (PH-004 testimony)
- Which mechanisms should hold THP and Tipper accountable? (PH-004 testimony)
- How does THP refuse engagements or offers that would corrupt inquiry without starving the institution? (THP-INSTITUTION-001)
- At what point, if any, does Tipper's commercial success create institutional risk — and how is that governed? (THP-INSTITUTION-001)
- What values and participation boundaries must THP itself refuse to compromise? (THP-PH-003)

3. How much behavioral consistency is necessary before a value is considered institutionalized?

5. Can will be measured without reducing it to persistence or productivity?
