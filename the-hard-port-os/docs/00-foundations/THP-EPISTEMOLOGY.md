# The Hard Port Epistemology

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non-canonical`  
> **Nature:** How THP understands knowledge — not what exists (ontology), not how research is conducted (methodology).

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines **how The Hard Port understands knowledge**: what counts as knowing, what limits knowing, and what obligations follow when THP claims to know something.

It inherits philosophical assumptions from [`THP-FOUNDATIONAL-PHILOSOPHY.md`](./THP-FOUNDATIONAL-PHILOSOPHY.md) and institutional scope from [`THP-INSTITUTIONAL-PURPOSE-AND-BOUNDARIES.md`](./THP-INSTITUTIONAL-PURPOSE-AND-BOUNDARIES.md).

It does **not**:

- define what reality, conscious beings, or systems **are** (ontology → philosophy document)
- define evidence scales, lifecycle states, or research workflows (methodology → [`research-methodology.md`](../knowledge-system/research-methodology.md), [`KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../../KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md))
- grant any claim authority by polish, repetition, or institutional tone

THP is a **learning institution**. Its epistemology must preserve the right — and the obligation — to be corrected.

## Scope

### Includes

- What THP means by knowledge
- Epistemic principles governing claims, confidence, scope, and revision
- How THP treats explanation, prediction, contradiction, and unknowns
- How epistemic obligations apply to translation surfaces (media, consulting, Tipper)

### Excludes

- Operational definitions of reality, beings, and systems
- Institutional mission, outputs, and boundaries
- Document types, folder placement, stage gates, and advancement procedure
- Field vocabularies for `evidence_level`, `status`, and `canonicality` (defined elsewhere)

## What THP Means by Knowledge

For THP, **knowledge** is a **justified, bounded, revisable model** of some aspect of reality that has earned enough support to guide inquiry, decision, or action within stated limits.

Knowledge is not:

- raw observation by itself,
- a strong opinion confidently stated,
- a framework because it has a diagram,
- Canon because it was reviewed once,
- truth in the sense of final, immutable correctness.

Knowledge **is** the institution's current best account of what the available evidence supports — including what the evidence does **not** support and where the account may fail.

## Foundational Epistemic Principles

1. **Models, not possession.** Knowing is maintaining useful models, not owning reality.
2. **Reality constrains.** No model is exempt from contact with what actually occurs.
3. **Scope is part of the claim.** A true-sounding statement outside its context is not knowledge.
4. **Confidence is explicit.** Strength of belief must be visible, not performed.
5. **Revision is integrity.** Updating a model after better evidence is success, not failure.
6. **Contradiction is signal.** Disconfirming evidence is information, not disloyalty.
7. **Unknowns are honest inventory.** What THP does not know must remain visible.
8. **Authority is earned and temporary.** Institutional weight follows review and evidence — and can be withdrawn.

## Knowledge Is a Model, Not Reality

THP treats all institutional claims as **models** — simplified representations useful for orientation and action.

A model may be:

- explanatory (why something happens),
- descriptive (what tends to occur),
- predictive (what should happen if conditions hold), or
- procedural (how to act under stated assumptions).

No model captures the whole of what it represents. A model that cannot specify what it leaves out is not yet knowledge. It is an untested compression.

## Reality as the Final Constraint

Reality, as defined in [`THP-FOUNDATIONAL-PHILOSOPHY.md`](./THP-FOUNDATIONAL-PHILOSOPHY.md), is the final constraint on knowledge.

THP does not treat consensus, eloquence, client satisfaction, media performance, or software adoption as substitutes for reality contact. Those may indicate translation success. They do not, by themselves, establish truth.

When model and outcome diverge, the model yields — or THP documents why divergence occurred and what would falsify the retained account.

## Scope and Context of Claims

Every knowledge claim carries implicit scope. THP requires scope to become **explicit**:

- **Context** — where, among whom, under what conditions
- **Boundary** — where the claim stops applying
- **Assumptions** — what must hold for the claim to remain plausible
- **Alternatives** — what competing explanations remain live

A claim without scope is not epistemically portable. It may persuade. It is not yet institutional knowledge.

## Confidence and Knowledge

**Confidence** is THP's graded expression of how strongly the current evidence supports a claim **within its stated scope**.

Confidence is:

- proportional to evidence quality and breadth, not to desire or delivery pressure
- separable from rhetorical tone
- revisable downward as well as upward

High confidence does not mean permanent truth. Low confidence does not mean worthlessness. Both states must remain legible in translation.

Operational scoring vocabularies live outside this document. Epistemically, confidence is a first-class property of every claim — not a cosmetic label applied after the fact.

## Revisability

All THP knowledge is **provisionally held**.

Revisability means:

- claims retain provenance to the evidence and decisions that supported them
- supersession is recorded, not hidden
- earlier versions remain accessible as history, not deleted as embarrassment

An institution that cannot revise without losing face is not a learning institution. It is a reputation institution pretending to know.

## Evidence Diversity

Evidence may arrive through multiple channels: direct observation, repeated pattern, structured measurement, bounded intervention, counterexample, contradiction, implementation feedback, and informed disagreement.

Epistemically, THP values **diversity of evidence type** over repetition of the same anecdote. Many similar stories without independent mechanism are one datum, not many.

This document does not define evidence levels or record types. It requires that evidence type and limitation be thinkable for every claim — not that every claim yet has them attached.

## Explanation vs Description

**Description** records what occurred without embedding unsupported causality.

**Explanation** proposes mechanism, context, and expected consequence — and states what would weaken the explanation.

THP treats unexplained regularity as a research opportunity, not as finished knowledge. Description may be accurate and still insufficient for action beyond the observed case.

Confusing description for explanation is a primary epistemological failure mode in consulting, media, and software automation.

## Prediction

A claim earns predictive weight when it specifies:

- what should happen if the model is useful,
- under which conditions,
- with what tolerance for error,
- and what outcome would reduce confidence.

Prediction is not prophecy. Failed prediction is valuable when recorded honestly. Unfalsifiable "prediction" is narrative, not knowledge.

## Contradictions

A **contradiction** is evidence that weakens, limits, or conflicts with a claim, model, or synthesis.

Epistemically, contradictions are:

- preserved, not smoothed over
- linked to the claim they challenge
- allowed to trigger revision at any level of institutional weight

The objective is not to win arguments. It is to reduce the institution's capacity to fool itself.

## Known Unknowns

**Known unknowns** are questions THP can name whose answers would materially change a model, decision, or translation.

Known unknowns are epistemic assets. They mark the frontier of honest inquiry. Hiding them to preserve authority is institutional self-harm.

THP distinguishes:

- **known unknowns** — identified gaps
- **unknown unknowns** — gaps not yet visible; humility without inventory
- **suppressed unknowns** — gaps intentionally concealed; epistemic violation

## The THP Knowledge Cycle

Epistemically — not procedurally — knowledge at THP moves through a repeating loop:

```text
Encounter reality
        ↓
Observe without premature explanation
        ↓
Form or revise a bounded model
        ↓
State scope, assumptions, and confidence
        ↓
Expose the model to contradiction and test
        ↓
Revise, limit, supersede, or reject
        ↓
Translate only what survives review
        ↓
Return new observations to the loop
```

This is an **epistemic cycle**, not a document workflow. How records move through folders and gates is defined in methodology and architecture documents.

## Levels of Knowing

THP distinguishes epistemic levels without collapsing them:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Noticing** | Something occurred; minimal interpretation attached |
| **Describing** | A stable pattern of occurrence is recorded with context |
| **Explaining** | A mechanism is proposed with falsifiable expectations |
| **Supporting** | Multiple evidence types converge within stated scope |
| **Institutional best current account** | Reviewed synthesis THP treats as authoritative **for now** |
| **Rejected or superseded** | Former account retained with reason for withdrawal |

**Canonical knowledge** — in the epistemic sense used here — is the institution's **current best explanation** within reviewed boundaries. It is not immutable truth. Canon may be revised when evidence, contradiction, or decision requires it.

Operational `canonicality` fields and Canon review procedure are defined elsewhere. Epistemically, Canon is provisional authority, not permanent doctrine.

## Institutional Skepticism

Institutional skepticism is THP's disciplined refusal to treat its own outputs as self-evidencing.

It applies to:

- popular frameworks
- repeated consulting narratives
- media-ready simplifications
- software patterns scaled before contradiction review
- internal prestige, seniority, or founder intuition

Skepticism is not cynicism. It is the institutional habit of asking: **What would change our mind? What are we not seeing? Who benefits if we stop asking?**

## Claims THP Will Not Make

THP will not claim:

- permanent truth for revisable models
- universal law from bounded observation
- causal certainty from anecdote alone
- client transformation as proof of institutional knowledge without recorded evidence
- software behavior as moral or metaphysical validation
- that absence of disproof equals proof
- that institutional authority replaces reality contact

THP may state what it currently believes, how strongly, within what scope, and what would weaken the belief. It will not perform certainty the repository does not support.

## Implications for Translation Systems (Tipper, Consulting, Media)

Translation surfaces inherit epistemic obligations:

| Surface | Epistemic obligation |
|---|---|
| **Media** | Preserve question state and evidence state; invite contradiction; do not collapse uncertainty for engagement |
| **Consulting** | Separate observation from diagnosis; record counterexamples; treat client outcomes as evidence, not proof of genius |
| **Tipper** | Operationalize supported patterns only; ask for missing evidence; return implementation outcomes to research |

No translation layer may upgrade epistemic status silently. A video, session, or feature carries knowledge; it does not **become** knowledge by distribution.

Tipper is a translation and observation instrument, not an epistemic authority. Software may surface evidence. It does not grant final institutional acceptance.

## Open Questions

- **Confidence without false precision.** How does THP express confidence clearly without implying numerical certainty where none exists?
- **Canon epistemics.** What additional epistemic gates must Canon synthesis satisfy beyond current review procedure?
- **Collective knowing.** When does a pattern across many small businesses become institutional knowledge rather than a stacked anecdote?
- **Translation decay.** How does THP detect when a simplified public version has drifted from the supported model it claims to represent?
- **Human vs system judgment.** Where may automated systems recommend action, and where must human acceptance remain explicit?
