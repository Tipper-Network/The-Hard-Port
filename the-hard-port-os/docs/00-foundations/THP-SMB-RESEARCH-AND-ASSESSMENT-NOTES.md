# THP SMB Research and Assessment Notes

> **Status:** `candidate` — operating notes, not Canon.

## Scope

Notes for THP's first operating surface: small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). Media observation, consultations, assessments, and clarifications apply this cycle.

Philosophical definitions: [`THP-REALITY-INTERPRETATION-SHARED-WORLD.md`](./THP-REALITY-INTERPRETATION-SHARED-WORLD.md), [`THP-SELF-INTENTION-EXPRESSION.md`](./THP-SELF-INTENTION-EXPRESSION.md), [`THP-VALUES-PRINCIPLES-WILL.md`](./THP-VALUES-PRINCIPLES-WILL.md). Entity assessment methods: [`../03-methods/entity-assessment/`](../03-methods/entity-assessment/). Institutional direction: [`THP-INSTITUTIONAL-PURPOSE-AND-BOUNDARIES.md`](./THP-INSTITUTIONAL-PURPOSE-AND-BOUNDARIES.md).

## Operating Cycle

Each SMB engagement — whether through media, assessment, or consultation — should trace:

```text
External conditions
        ↓
Experience
        ↓
Interpretation
        ↓
Intention
        ↓
Expression
        ↓
Consequences
        ↓
Revision
```

## Stage Notes

| Stage | SMB research / assessment question |
|---|---|
| **External conditions** | What market, operational, relational, and structural conditions exist independent of the owner's current story? |
| **Experience** | What did the owner, team, or customers actually encounter — not what they wish had occurred? |
| **Interpretation** | What meaning, explanation, or blame structure is being applied? Is it accurate within scope? |
| **Intention** | What was the owner or entity trying to produce — explicitly or implicitly? Assess across four dimensions (see below); do not auto-map to goal, vision, mission, or brand. |
| **Expression** | What actions, communications, offers, or designs were deployed? |
| **Consequences** | What outcomes followed — including unintended effects and counterexamples? |
| **Revision** | What should change in action, interpretation, goal, or relationship to the outcome? |

## Distinctions to Preserve

- **Subjective experience vs external accuracy** — honor the owner's experience; test claims against observable conditions
- **Facts vs agreement** — team consensus or client belief does not establish fact
- **Effectiveness vs legitimacy** — a tactic may work and still harm or impose unjustifiably
- **Responsibility vs total causal control** — claiming responsibility for learning is not claiming sole causation of every outcome

## Four Dimensions of Entity Intention

When assessing an entity (not only the owner's personal intention), record:

| Dimension | SMB assessment question |
|---|---|
| **stated_intention** | What does the entity explicitly claim it wants to accomplish or contribute? |
| **governing_intention** | What purpose, priorities, incentives, and commitments consistently guide decisions? |
| **enacted_intention** | What direction is revealed through attention, authority, resources, policies, and repeated actions? |
| **perceived_intention** | What do customers, workers, or communities understand the entity to be pursuing? |

Contradiction among dimensions is a primary signal. Full definitions and explicit non-mapping to goal/vision/mission/brand: [`ENTITY-INTENTION-DIMENSIONS.md`](../03-methods/entity-assessment/ENTITY-INTENTION-DIMENSIONS.md). Operational loop: [`ENTITY-INTENTION-EXPRESSION-LOOP.md`](../03-methods/entity-assessment/ENTITY-INTENTION-EXPRESSION-LOOP.md).

Use **organizational identity** operationally; **organizational consciousness** remains a [research hypothesis](../../research/hypotheses/HYP-002-organizational-consciousness.md) only.

## Values, Principles, and Responsibility Loop

For value-aligned assessment, use the candidate loop in [`ENTITY-VALUES-PRINCIPLES-LOOP.md`](../03-methods/entity-assessment/ENTITY-VALUES-PRINCIPLES-LOOP.md):

```text
Beliefs and experience → values → principles → conditions and trade-offs
→ choice and decision → action or non-action → consequences
→ responsibility and reflection → revision
```

Record assessment fields: `declared_values`, `governing_priorities`, `operating_principles`, `observed_actions`, `experienced_consequences`, `value_principle_contradictions`, `stakeholder_perceptions`.

Engagement refusal and authority boundaries: [`ENTITY-ENGAGEMENT-BOUNDARIES.md`](../03-methods/entity-assessment/ENTITY-ENGAGEMENT-BOUNDARIES.md).
