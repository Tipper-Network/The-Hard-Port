# THP Founding Questions

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non-canonical`  
> **Nature:** Enduring questions produced by the founding origin — not settled answers.

## Purpose of This Document

This document lists the **ten enduring founding questions** generated by The Hard Port's origin.

It does not reproduce founder testimony. The institutional synthesis and lived origin are recorded in [`source-records/THP-FQ-001-FOUNDING-ORIGIN.md`](./source-records/THP-FQ-001-FOUNDING-ORIGIN.md). Raw form responses are preserved in [`THP-FQ-001.md`](./THP-FQ-001.md).

These questions enter THP's philosophy and research agenda. They are not finalized institutional conclusions.

## Enduring Founding Questions

1. Why does sustained effort frequently fail to become meaningful progress?

2. How does internal intention become coherent external action and communication?

3. What conditions help or prevent people from understanding their own decisions?

4. How should responsibility be divided between individuals and the institutions shaping their choices?

5. What important dimensions of human experience remain unrepresented when entities evaluate success?

6. How can entities genuinely consider the people and communities around them rather than merely measure them?

7. How do communication, trust, exchange, and mutual dependence produce durable forms of coordination?

8. How can understanding become action, learning, and eventually wisdom?

9. How can THP study entities while also intervening in their development?

10. How can Tipper codify THP's methods without controlling the conclusions THP is permitted to reach?
