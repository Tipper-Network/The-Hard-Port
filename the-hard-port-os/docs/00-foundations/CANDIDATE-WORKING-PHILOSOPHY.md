# Candidate Working Philosophy — Freeze Registry

> **Frozen:** 2026-07-21  
> **Policy:** THP-PH-001 through THP-PH-004 are **candidate working philosophy**. They are usable for THP research, assessment, consulting design, and Tipper boundaries — but are **not Canon** and **not closed**.

## What frozen means

1. **No new philosophy forms** in `active/CURRENT_TASK.md`.
2. **Unresolved questions** live in [`OPEN-PHILOSOPHICAL-QUESTIONS.md`](./OPEN-PHILOSOPHICAL-QUESTIONS.md) — not scattered across session tasks.
3. **Operating evidence sharpens philosophy** — SMB work, media, and first interventions may produce observations that update candidate positions through the normal knowledge lifecycle.
4. **Amendments require evidence or explicit decision** — not additional preliminary Q&A forms by default.
