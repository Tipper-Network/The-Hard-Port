# Entity Maturity Model

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non_canonical`  
> **Scope:** Entity-level maturity for founder-led microbusinesses. Levels 1–2 are **active definitions**. Levels 3–7 are **unvalidated proposals**.

This model is distinct from the domain-based maturity in [`layer-03-business-maturity-model.md`](../../../intelligence/layer-03-business-maturity-model.md). That layer assesses capability by domain; this model describes overall entity operating stage for the first onboarder path.

## Principles

1. A level represents **demonstrated operating condition**, not revenue, follower count, or founder ambition.
2. Only Levels 1 and 2 are active institutional definitions until validated by operating evidence.
3. **Do not treat community stability, brand awareness, growth, and scaling as one maturity dimension** until they are separated and tested.
4. Social-media activity is an operating-environment signal — not proof of maturity level.
5. Regression is possible under founder absence, market shock, or loss of routines.

## Active Definitions

### Level 1 — Founder-Dependent Survival

**ID:** `level_1_founder_dependent_survival`

The business exists and performs real activity, but its continuation depends heavily on the founder's daily intervention.

#### Observable conditions

- Work and decisions concentrate around the founder.
- Priorities change with the latest urgency or platform trend.
- Customer acquisition is inconsistent or poorly understood.
- Knowledge of what works is mostly tacit and unrepeated.
- Content and communication are irregular relative to stated goals.
- Repeated experiments occur without consolidated learning.
- Immediate revenue pressure dominates planning.
- Weak separation between founder identity and entity identity.

#### Exit criteria (toward Level 2)

- A defined offering exists and can be described consistently.
- At least one repeatable routine produces a known outcome.
- The founder can articulate current position without external prompting alone.
- Basic operating information is available for assessment.
- The entity has not been abandoned despite frustration.

### Level 2 — Repeatable Operational Stability

**ID:** `level_2_repeatable_operational_stability`

The business has established enough coherence to operate beyond immediate survival.

#### Observable conditions

- A defined offering with clearer boundaries.
- A clearer customer or community relationship — not merely an audience.
- Repeatable operating routines that survive a normal week without constant reinvention.
- Measurable priorities — even if metrics are simple.
- A more coherent operating narrative.
- Defined responsibilities — even if many remain with the founder.
- Ability to learn from recurring activity and adjust.

#### What Level 2 is not

- Ready to scale
- Strong brand awareness
- Stable community at scale
- Proof of growth trajectory
- Social-media performance excellence

Level 2 means the entity has a **stable enough base** from which intentional growth can begin — not that growth, community, or brand objectives are achieved.

#### Exit criteria (toward proposed Level 3 — unvalidated)

Not defined institutionally. Requires separation of growth, community, and stability dimensions before activation.

## Unvalidated Proposed Levels

The following levels are **founder-proposed sketches** from [`THP-SMB-001.md`](../../../businesses/THP-SMB-001.md). They are **not active definitions**. They conflate separate dimensions (community stability, brand awareness, growth, scaling) that must be separated and tested before institutional adoption.

| Level | Proposed label (unvalidated) | Founder notes | Conflation risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3** | Growth attempt / instability | Attempting growth but not fully stable | Mixes growth intent with stability |
| **4** | Named growth | Growing; "proper name"; requires community growth | Mixes brand, community, growth |
| **5** | Stable growth | Stable and growing | Undefined stability vs growth criteria |
| **6** | Scale preparation | Ready for scale; requires strong brand awareness | Mixes brand, scale readiness, community |
| **7** | Scaling | Active scaling | No separated operational criteria |

**Do not use Levels 3–7 for client assessment, media targeting, or Tipper readiness gates** until:

- community stability,
- brand awareness,
- growth trajectory, and
- scaling capability

are defined as **separate observable dimensions** with evidence criteria.

## First Onboarder Transition

The first THP onboarder is targeted at:

```text
Level 1 (Founder-Dependent Survival)
        ↓
Level 2 (Repeatable Operational Stability)
```
