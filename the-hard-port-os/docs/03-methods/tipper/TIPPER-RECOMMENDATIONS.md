# Tipper Recommendations

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non_canonical`

## Identity Boundary

Tipper may support voluntary identity and lifestyle exploration, but it must not claim authority over a user's identity or secretly infer personal values and intentions without informed permission.

## Scope

Recommendations connected to stated interests, voluntary participation, and supported patterns — not covert identity steering.

## Permitted

- Recommendations tied to user-stated interests or confirmed preferences
- Pattern-based business recommendations per [`layer-08-tipper-intelligence-framework.md`](../../../intelligence/layer-08-tipper-intelligence-framework.md) eligibility gates
- Visible reasoning, confidence, and rejection controls
- Lifestyle exploration routes the user can dismiss

## Prohibited

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|
| Recommendations based on hidden-value inference without consent | **Prohibited** |
| Recommendations framed as revealing a user's true identity or destiny | **Prohibited** |
| Undisclosed psychological profiling driving recommendations | **Prohibited** |
| Irreversible inferred identity labels affecting recommendation tiers | **Prohibited** |

Recommendations suggest possibilities. They do not declare who the user is or where they must go.

## User Controls

Users must see why a recommendation appeared, reject it, and correct underlying preference data.
