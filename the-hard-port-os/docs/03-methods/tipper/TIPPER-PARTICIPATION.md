# Tipper Participation

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non_canonical`

## Identity Boundary

Tipper may support voluntary identity and lifestyle exploration, but it must not claim authority over a user's identity or secretly infer personal values and intentions without informed permission.

## Scope

Recording and reflecting voluntary participation in communities, activities, and entities.

## Permitted

- User-initiated participation records
- Opt-in community membership
- Reflection on experiences the user chooses to log
- Skill discovery linked to activities the user participated in voluntarily

## Prohibited

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|
| Using participation to infer hidden values without consent | **Prohibited** |
| Building undisclosed psychological profiles from participation patterns | **Prohibited** |
| Assigning irreversible identity labels from participation history | **Prohibited** |
| Treating participation as proof of a user's true identity | **Prohibited** |

## Preference Inference

When may Tipper infer preferences from participation? **Open question** (THP-PH-002 §21). Until resolved:

- inferences must be provisional, visible, correctable, removable
- informed permission required before using participation for value or identity inference
- participation data must not silently upgrade to identity authority
