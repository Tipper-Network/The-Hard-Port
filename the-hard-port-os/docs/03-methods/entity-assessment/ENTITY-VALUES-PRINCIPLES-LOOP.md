# Entity Values, Principles, and Responsibility Loop

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non_canonical`  
> **Philosophical source:** [`THP-PH-003`](../../00-foundations/THP-VALUES-PRINCIPLES-WILL.md) (`THP-VALUES-PRINCIPLES-WILL.md`)

This document records the **operational loop** and **assessment fields** only. It does not restate the full philosophical theory of values, will, or responsibility.

## Operating Loop

```text
Beliefs and experience
        ↓
Values
        ↓
Principles
        ↓
Conditions and trade-offs
        ↓
Choice and decision
        ↓
Action or non-action
        ↓
Consequences
        ↓
Responsibility and reflection
        ↓
Revision
```

Elements may recur, interrupt, or run in smaller sub-loops. Non-action may be intentional. The loop does not assume every action originates consciously from a stated value.

## Assessment Fields

Record these fields during entity assessment, consultation, or media analysis:

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| **declared_values** | Values the entity explicitly states or publishes |
| **governing_priorities** | What appears to actually govern trade-offs, resource allocation, and repeated decisions |
| **operating_principles** | Rules, criteria, or methods the entity uses to translate values into conduct |
| **observed_actions** | Actions and non-actions visible within assessment scope |
| **experienced_consequences** | Outcomes experienced by the entity and affected parties |
| **value_principle_contradictions** | Documented conflicts among declared values, principles, actions, or consequences |
| **stakeholder_perceptions** | How authorized stakeholders and affected parties understand what the entity prioritizes |

## Stage Notes

| Loop stage | Assessment focus |
|---|---|
| **Beliefs and experience** | What propositions does the entity operate as if true? What experience shaped them? Distinguish belief from desire per THP-PH-003 §2. |
| **Values** | What is declared? What appears to govern? Do not treat style or branding language as values by default. |
| **Principles** | How are values translated into recurring conduct? Are principles connected to identified values? |
| **Conditions and trade-offs** | What constraints, conflicts, and sacrifices are visible? Context informs but does not decide. |
| **Choice and decision** | Who decided? With what authority? Under what information limits? |
| **Action or non-action** | What was done or withheld? Was withholding deliberate? |
| **Consequences** | Intended and unintended effects on entity and affected parties. |
| **Responsibility and reflection** | Who accepts relevant consequences? Is revision occurring? |
| **Revision** | What beliefs, priorities, principles, or actions changed — or were refused? |

## Terminology Boundaries

Do not use this loop to infer:

| Prohibited inference | Institutional reference |
|---|---|
| Belief as desire | THP-PH-003 §2 |
| Value as unchangeable belief | THP-PH-003 §3 |
| Style as value | THP-PH-003 §4 |
| Measurement as proof of valuation | THP-PH-003 §6 |
| Repeated action as proof of a true value | THP-PH-003 §7 |

When stated values and repeated actions conflict, record `value_principle_contradictions` for investigation — do not conclude which represents the entity's permanent true value.

## Relationship to Intention Loop

Use alongside [`ENTITY-INTENTION-EXPRESSION-LOOP.md`](./ENTITY-INTENTION-EXPRESSION-LOOP.md) and [`ENTITY-INTENTION-DIMENSIONS.md`](./ENTITY-INTENTION-DIMENSIONS.md). Intention dimensions describe directional commitment; this loop describes how values and principles translate into conduct and consequences.

## Boundaries

- **Individual authority** — authority to retain, revise, or reject personal values remains with the individual (THP-PH-003 §13).
- **Entity authority** — organizational value direction requires legitimately authorized stakeholders.
- **Harmony** — coherence may coexist with visible tension; do not treat harmony as absence of trade-offs (THP-PH-003 §9).
- **Will** — do not equate will with constant growth or absence of friction (THP-PH-003 §10).
