# Entity Intention and Expression Loop

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non_canonical`  
> **Philosophical source:** [`THP-PH-002`](../../00-foundations/THP-SELF-INTENTION-EXPRESSION.md) (`THP-SELF-INTENTION-EXPRESSION.md`)

This document records the **operational loop** and its assessment implications only. It does not restate the full philosophical theory of the self.

## Operating Loop

```text
Conditions and internal signals
↕
Awareness and attention
↕
Interpretation and existing patterns
↕
Values, principles, intention, and impulse
↕
Choice and decision
↕
Action or non-action
↕
Expression and consequence
↕
Reflection, responsibility, and pattern revision
```

Elements may recur, interrupt, or run in smaller sub-loops. Non-action may be intentional.

## Assessment Implications

| Loop stage | Assessment focus |
|---|---|
| **Conditions and internal signals** | What external and internal conditions are present? What is outside stakeholder-approved scope? |
| **Awareness and attention** | What is the entity actually attending to — versus what it claims to prioritize? |
| **Interpretation and patterns** | What explanations, blame structures, or narratives are applied? Are they accurate within scope? |
| **Values, principles, intention** | Compare [`stated_intention`](./ENTITY-INTENTION-DIMENSIONS.md), `governing_intention`, `enacted_intention`, and `perceived_intention`. |
| **Choice and decision** | Who decided? With what information? Under what constraints? |
| **Action or non-action** | What was done or withheld? Was withholding deliberate? |
| **Expression and consequence** | Distinguish intended, observable, received, and interpreted expression. Record unintended effects. |
| **Reflection and revision** | Does the entity revise when consequences contradict expectations? Who accepts responsibility? |

## Boundaries

- **Individual direction** remains under individual authority; THP analysis is not a decision.
- **Entity direction** requires stakeholder-approved governance; THP analysis is separated until adopted.
- **Growth is not required** for intentionality — preservation, withdrawal, rest, and closure may be intentional.
- **Non-action may be intentional** — do not treat inactivity as absence of direction by default.
- Use **organizational identity** operationally; organizational consciousness remains a [research hypothesis](../../../research/hypotheses/HYP-002-organizational-consciousness.md).

## Consent

Explicit consent is required before examining personal history, goals, interests, values, intentions, identity questions, vulnerabilities, or desired futures. See THP-PH-002 §18.
