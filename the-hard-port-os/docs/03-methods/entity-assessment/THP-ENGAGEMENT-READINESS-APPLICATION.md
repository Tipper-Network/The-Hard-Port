# THP Engagement Readiness Application

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non_canonical` — **placeholder**. Form fields and scoring are not yet designed.

## Purpose

Test whether a founder-led microbusiness is potentially ready for THP engagement before committing to the Entity Position and Direction Assessment.

## What the Application Tests

The application evaluates:

- **Openness** — willingness to examine assumptions, patterns, and the founder's participation in outcomes
- **Responsibility** — capacity to acknowledge that the current approach may be insufficient without abandoning the entity
- **Operating history** — evidence the business is already operating, not merely planned
- **Information availability** — ability and willingness to provide relevant operating information honestly
- **Willingness to participate** — readiness for structured assessment, measurement, and follow-through

## Explicit Boundaries

The application **must not** require applicants to:

- describe themselves as incompetent;
- label themselves as failures;
- declare themselves bad business owners;
- perform self-condemnation to qualify;
- accept THP's interpretation before assessment occurs.

Readiness is based on **openness and responsibility** — not shame, humiliation, or predetermined guilt.

An applicant may acknowledge frustration, insufficient results, and willingness to examine their approach without being asked to adopt a global negative self-judgment.

## What the Application Is Not

- A marketing lead form optimized for volume
- A psychological screening
- A social-media follower threshold check
- A requirement to admit the business model is "bad" before THP will engage
- A substitute for the Entity Position and Direction Assessment

## Relationship to Refusal

Failure to meet readiness criteria results in **delay or refusal of engagement** — not a judgment that the applicant lacks legitimate worth, values, or potential.

See [`ENTITY-ENGAGEMENT-BOUNDARIES.md`](./ENTITY-ENGAGEMENT-BOUNDARIES.md).
