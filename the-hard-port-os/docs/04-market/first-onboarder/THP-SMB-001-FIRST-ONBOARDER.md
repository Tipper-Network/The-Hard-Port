# The First THP Onboarder

> **Status:** `candidate`, `non_canonical`, `founder_hypothesis`  
> **Primary transition:** Level 1 → Level 2 ([`ENTITY-MATURITY-MODEL.md`](../../02-ontology/entity-maturity/ENTITY-MATURITY-MODEL.md))

## 1. Target Entity

The first THP onboarder is a **founder-led microbusiness** whose customer acquisition, communication, and presence depend heavily on the internet.

The business is **already operating**. It has produced an offering, attempted to attract customers, and experienced some form of market interaction.

It is **not merely an idea**.

The founder remains central to most decisions, communication, production, and problem-solving.

The entity is still operating in survival mode and is attempting to move toward repeatable operational stability.

**Distinction:** The target is an operating microbusiness with market interaction — not an untested concept, side project without customers, or purely aspirational venture.

## 2. Current Maturity Position

### Level 1 — Founder-Dependent Survival

The business exists and performs real activity, but its continuation depends heavily on the founder's daily intervention.

Typical characteristics include:

- unclear or changing priorities;
- inconsistent customer acquisition;
- reactive decision-making;
- limited operational documentation;
- weak separation between founder and entity;
- irregular content and communication;
- limited understanding of which actions produce results;
- repeated experimentation without consolidated learning;
- and pressure to generate immediate revenue.

### Level 2 — Repeatable Operational Stability

The business has established enough coherence to operate beyond immediate survival.

It possesses:

- a defined offering;
- a clearer customer or community relationship;
- repeatable operating routines;
- measurable priorities;
- a more coherent narrative;
- defined responsibilities;
- and an ability to learn from recurring activity.

Level 2 does **not** mean the business is ready to scale. It means the entity has developed a stable enough base from which intentional growth can begin.

Full definitions: [`ENTITY-MATURITY-MODEL.md`](../../02-ontology/entity-maturity/ENTITY-MATURITY-MODEL.md).

## 3. What the Founder Has Already Tried

Before approaching THP, the founder has commonly attempted:

- consistent posting;
- trend-based content;
- paid boosting;
- visual branding;
- hiring social-media or creative professionals;
- increasing production;
- and trying multiple disconnected approaches.

The problem is not a lack of activity.

It is the absence of a coherent relationship among:

- the business objective;
- the entity's identity;
- the customer or community;
- the selected activity;
- the expected consequence;
- and the measurement used to evaluate it.

Social-media activity is part of the **operating environment**, not the central definition of business health.

## 4. The Problem the Founder Believes They Have

The founder may believe:

- the business model is failing;
- people cannot see or understand the work;
- the algorithms are preventing growth;
- the current production structure cannot scale;
- or more marketing activity is required.

These explanations may contain truth, but THP should not accept them without examination.

## 5. THP's Initial Hypothesis

THP begins from the hypothesis that:

> Starting a business and managing an operating entity require different capabilities.

The founder may possess enough creativity, courage, skill, or commitment to create the business without yet possessing the systems required to manage it.

The deeper problem may therefore involve:

- undefined objectives;
- weak measurement;
- founder–entity confusion;
- unclear decision criteria;
- fragmented routines;
- inconsistent communication;
- limited customer consideration;
- or an inability to consolidate learning from previous attempts.

This remains a **diagnostic hypothesis**, not a conclusion applied to every client.

## 6. Readiness Indicators

An owner is potentially ready for THP when:

- they are frustrated with the present condition but have not abandoned the entity;
- they have made multiple attempts to improve;
- those attempts have not produced coherent or repeatable results;
- they can acknowledge that their current approach is insufficient;
- they are willing to examine their own participation in the outcome;
- they can provide information honestly;
- and they are open to changing routines, assumptions, or structures.

Readiness requires **responsibility and openness** — not agreement with every THP interpretation.

**Distinction:** Readiness is based on openness and responsibility, not self-condemnation. The application must not require the founder to describe themselves as incompetent, failures, or bad business owners.

Application placeholder: [`THP-ENGAGEMENT-READINESS-APPLICATION.md`](../../03-methods/entity-assessment/THP-ENGAGEMENT-READINESS-APPLICATION.md).

## 7. Delay or Refusal Indicators

THP should delay or refuse an engagement when the owner:

- insists that all problems originate outside the entity;
- demands a predetermined solution without investigation;
- seeks only temporary symptom relief;
- refuses to provide relevant information;
- rejects measurement or follow-through;
- expects THP to operate the business on their behalf;
- is unwilling to change any behavior or structure;
- or lacks enough operating activity for a meaningful assessment.

Refusal is not a judgment that the owner's entire value system is wrong. See [`ENTITY-ENGAGEMENT-BOUNDARIES.md`](../../03-methods/entity-assessment/ENTITY-ENGAGEMENT-BOUNDARIES.md).

## 8. First Interaction Outcome

After the first THP interaction, the owner should understand:

- the current maturity position of the entity;
- why continuous activity has not necessarily become progress;
- the distinction between creating the business and managing it;
- which patterns are maintaining the current position;
- how THP will structure experimentation and learning;
- and how daily operations can be converted into visible levels, capabilities, routines, and feedback loops.

The game-like framing should make progress visible.

It must not trivialize the financial, personal, or operational risks faced by the founder.

## 9. First Paid Engagement

The first paid engagement is the **Entity Position and Direction Assessment**.

Method: [`ENTITY-POSITION-AND-DIRECTION-ASSESSMENT.md`](../../03-methods/entity-assessment/ENTITY-POSITION-AND-DIRECTION-ASSESSMENT.md).

Its purpose is to establish where the founder and entity currently stand, where they are attempting to go, and what prevents coherent movement between those positions.

**Boundary:** The first engagement is **diagnostic and directional** — not unlimited implementation, transformation guarantee, or ongoing operation of the business on the client's behalf.

The client receives:

### Current-Position Assessment

A structured account of:

- the founder's role;
- the entity's operating condition;
- its maturity level;
- current offering;
- customer or community relationship;
- decision patterns;
- existing routines;
- and present constraints.

### Intention and Alignment Assessment

A comparison among:

- stated intention;
- governing intention;
- enacted intention;
- perceived intention;
- declared values;
- operating principles;
- and repeated actions.

### Entity Narrative

A clear business-language explanation of:

- what the entity is;
- who it serves;
- why its work matters;
- what position it currently occupies;
- and what direction it is pursuing.

This is an operating narrative, not necessarily a complete branding engagement.

### Distance Map

A documented distinction between:

- present position;
- desired Level 2 condition;
- capabilities already present;
- missing capabilities;
- contradictions;
- and immediate constraints.

### Initial Operating Plan

A limited action system containing:

- the first priorities;
- defined actions;
- relevant measurements;
- responsibilities;
- feedback checkpoints;
- and a review point.

The assessment does not promise complete transformation. It produces a grounded position and an intentional next movement.

## 10. Research Produced for THP

THP should learn:

- how founders understand themselves;
- how they describe their entities;
- how they understand the relationship between founder and entity;
- which problems they identify;
- which conditions they overlook;
- which values they claim;
- which principles guide actual decisions;
- how repeated actions differ from stated intentions;
- what they believe customers want;
- how they respond to contradiction;
- and which interventions improve operational coherence.

The research record should distinguish:

- founder testimony;
- THP observation;
- operational data;
- interpretation;
- hypothesis;
- intervention;
- and consequence.

## 11. Readiness for Tipper

The entity becomes a candidate for Tipper when it has **observable** entity and community conditions:

- an identifiable organizational identity;
- a coherent offering;
- a defined direction;
- explicit operating responsibilities;
- recurring customers, members, or participants;
- an identifiable community around the entity;
- willingness to invite structured participation and feedback;
- and sufficient stability to maintain that relationship.

The founder must also understand that durable development is not produced solely through individual effort.

The entity's direction, reputation, learning, and growth are influenced by the people who participate around it.

Tipper becomes relevant when the entity is prepared to make that relationship visible, structured, and reciprocal.

**Distinction:** Tipper readiness is defined through observable entity and community conditions — not founder aspiration, posting volume, or follower count alone.

## 12. Strategic Boundary

THP does not exist merely to improve social-media performance.

It may work through current digital platforms because those platforms are part of the entity's operating environment.

However, the purpose is to help the entity become more coherent in:

- what it is;
- what it does;
- whom it serves;
- how it decides;
- how it communicates;
- and how it learns from the people around it.

Content performance is one signal within that system — not the definition of business health.

## Media Attraction Path

Media audience definition (Layer 09): founder-led online microbusinesses that have already attempted content, branding, promotion, or professional support but remain unable to convert their activity into coherent and repeatable business progress.
