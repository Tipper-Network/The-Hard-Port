# Templates

## What Belongs Here

Reusable document structures and metadata standards.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Completed records, accepted decisions, live system specifications, or Canon chapters.

## How Documents Enter

A template enters when multiple future documents require a stable structure or vocabulary.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

Templates are versioned. Replacement templates identify migration requirements; old versions move to `../archive/` only after existing documents remain interpretable.
