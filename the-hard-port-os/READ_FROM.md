The maturity work now stops at Level 3.

What comes next

Immediate next task: build the first-onboarder diagnostic

We need an instrument that allows THP to examine a business and determine:

Whether it qualifies as a Level 1 business.
Which Level 1 conditions are most severe.
Which Level 2 gates are missing.
Whether the owner is ready for THP’s intervention.
What evidence must be collected during onboarding.
What THP should work on first.

The diagnostic should not merely produce a score like “42 out of 100.”

It should produce a structured conclusion such as:

This is a Level 1 business. Its strongest area is demand evidence. Its main barriers to Level 2 are customer understanding, financial visibility, and founder dependency. The first intervention should clarify the core customer and offer before increasing content or acquisition activity.

THP sales calls.
Client qualification.
Onboarding.
Service design.
Research collection.
Progress measurement.
YouTube calls to action.
Tipper integration.
The sequence after the diagnostic

Diagnostic → Level 1-to-2 intervention → service structure → onboarding journey → YouTube attraction system
