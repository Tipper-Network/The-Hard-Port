# Economy

## What Belongs Here

Operational models for value exchange, transactions, allocation, incentives, and economic state.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Pricing copy, speculative economic philosophy, or unaccepted commercial models.

Research evidence remains in `../../research/`; proposals remain in `../../rfcs/`; decision rationale remains in `../../decisions/`.

## How Documents Enter

A system specification enters after its operating boundary is defined, relevant decisions are linked, and the document describes current reality using `../SYSTEM_TEMPLATE.md`.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

Accepted changes create a new version or replacement specification. Superseded documents retain reciprocal links and move to `../../archive/` only when no longer operational.

## Related Folders

- `../` contains the systems index and template.
- `../../research/` supplies evidence.
- `../../rfcs/` proposes changes.
- `../../decisions/` records accepted institutional decisions.
- `../../adrs/` records technical architecture decisions.
- `../../sops/` defines execution.
- `../../canon/` synthesizes accepted knowledge.
