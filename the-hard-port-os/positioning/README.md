# Positioning

Company-wide narrative, audience, transformation, promise, and communication strategy.

These documents translate working models and Canon material into language used by content, services, and client-facing material. They do not override `../systems/`, accepted decisions, or `../canon/`.

## What Belongs Here

Audience definitions, transformation narratives, positioning, promises, and downstream communication strategy.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Research evidence, operational specifications, Canon chapters, or unsupported claims disguised as messaging.

## How Documents Enter

Positioning enters when its source model is named and its intended audience and use are clear.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

Positioning is revised when source models, decisions, or Canon claims change. Superseded narratives retain links or move to `../archive/`.
