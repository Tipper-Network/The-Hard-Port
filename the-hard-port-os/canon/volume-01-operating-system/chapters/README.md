# Additional Chapters

## What Belongs Here

Future Volume I chapters whose scope does not duplicate the named chapter structure and has passed architectural review.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Convenience notes, unreviewed source material, or chapters created only to avoid resolving scope overlap.

## How Documents Enter

Use `../../../templates/CANON_CHAPTER_TEMPLATE.md`, identify sources, and add the chapter to the volume README.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

Accepted restructuring may promote, merge, rename, or supersede a chapter while preserving history and links.

## Related Folders

- `../` contains the named Volume I chapters.
- `../../../research/` contains working evidence.
- `../../../archive/` preserves superseded material.
