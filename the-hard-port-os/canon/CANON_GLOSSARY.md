# Canon Glossary

## Purpose

Maintain terms whose meaning must remain consistent across Canon volumes.

This seed contains only repository-governance terms. Philosophical, organizational, business, and protocol definitions remain TODO until source review.

## Terms

### Canon

The authoritative synthesis layer containing reviewed, source-backed knowledge accepted within a stated scope and version.

### Canonicality

Whether the Canon may treat a document or claim as authoritative. See `DOCUMENT_METADATA_STANDARD.md`.

### Source Document

A working research, RFC, decision, system, SOP, archive, or other document cited by a Canon chapter. Citation does not make the source canonical.

### Working Layer

The repository areas where knowledge is investigated, proposed, decided, specified, executed, challenged, and revised.

### System Specification

A versioned description of current operational reality, including state, rules, roles, interfaces, governance, and validation.

### Decision Record

A durable account of an accepted or proposed institutional choice, rationale, alternatives, consequences, and review conditions.
