# RFC-001 — Tipper as Community Infrastructure

Status: Draft  
Owner: The Hard Port  
Created: 2026-07-18  
Source: Session 01

## Proposal

Investigate whether the canonical Tipper Intelligence Framework should expand beyond recommendations and intervention support to include infrastructure for community relationships, permissions, governance, and feedback loops.

Proposed principle:

> Tipper is not the purpose. It is infrastructure through which supported knowledge may become a living, governed system and return new evidence.

## Motivation

Session 01 introduced three claims not fully represented in Layer 08:

1. Every entity develops culture.
2. Every community develops rules.
3. Tipper may provide relationships, permissions, governance, and feedback infrastructure through which communities evolve.

Layer 08 already defines Tipper as the operationalization and evidence-return stage of the knowledge engine. The unresolved question is whether community infrastructure belongs inside that role or represents a separate product and governance boundary.

## Evidence

Current evidence level: `E0 — Assumption`

No business observations, community studies, interventions, or software outcomes currently support the expansion.

Existing conceptual support:

- [`intelligence/layer-00-knowledge-engine.md`](../intelligence/layer-00-knowledge-engine.md) defines Tipper as operationalization and observation infrastructure.
- [`intelligence/layer-08-tipper-intelligence-framework.md`](../intelligence/layer-08-tipper-intelligence-framework.md) defines human oversight, pattern promotion, recommendations, and evidence return.
- [`intelligence/layer-09-research-initiative.md`](../intelligence/layer-09-research-initiative.md) treats community as a relevant small-business system.

## Counterarguments

- Community governance may be a separate product problem from consulting intelligence.
- Infrastructure may impose rules rather than merely support emergent community behavior.
- “Every entity develops culture” is too broad to guide product scope.
- Building permissions and governance before a validated community use case would violate Layer 08's promotion rules.
- The expansion could turn Tipper into an undefined platform rather than a bounded operationalization layer.

## Required Research

- Define “entity,” “community,” “culture,” and “governance” operationally.
- Observe existing community rules inside small businesses.
- Identify a human intervention that improves a measurable community outcome.
- Establish what software support is required after the human intervention works.
- Define governance decisions that must remain human.
- Record failure modes, power concentration, and unintended incentives.

## Open Questions

- Is Tipper infrastructure for individual businesses, cross-business communities, or both?
- Which relationships and permissions would it own?
- Who has authority to create or change community rules?
- Which outcomes demonstrate that governance infrastructure creates value?
- What evidence threshold should be required before changing Layer 08?
- Does this belong in Tipper or in a separate future system?

## Decision

Pending.

Do not modify Layer 08 until this RFC has evidence, a bounded use case, and an explicit decision.
