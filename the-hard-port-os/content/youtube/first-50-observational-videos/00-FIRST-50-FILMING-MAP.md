## Purpose

Record THP's first public diagnostic layer through **observation**, not instruction.

The audience should discover the worldview through repeated observation rather than being taught the full thesis immediately.

Do not introduce the full THP solution too early. The audience first needs to recognize the condition.

---

## What the First Fifty Establish

The videos should gradually establish that:

1. Small businesses are overwhelmed.
2. They respond by increasing activity.
3. More activity creates more complexity.
4. Marketing tools can temporarily hide the deeper condition.
5. Customers, knowledge, identity, and relationships remain disconnected.
6. Growth requires more than visibility.
7. The business must become capable of remembering, learning, and relating.

## Narrative Progression (Viewer Journey)

```text
Overwhelm → Reaction → Misdiagnosis → Disconnection → Recognition
```

Publishing order of foundational videos may differ from narrative order. Filming is grouped by **drift** (train of thought). Editorial assembly follows narrative progression.

## Publishing Rhythm (Post-Production)

Between every foundational video, release approximately **three** supporting observations:

```text
FND → OBS ×3 → FND → OBS ×3 → …
```

Editorial narrative order (recommended release sequence for foundational videos):

1. FND-001 → 2. FND-004 → 3. FND-007 → 4. FND-010 → 5. FND-003 → 6. FND-005 → 7. FND-008 → 8. FND-011 → 9. FND-006 → 10. FND-009 → 11. FND-012 → 12. FND-002

Adjust after field evidence. Supporting OBS clips slot between according to drift source and narrative fit.

## Voice and Delivery Rules

| Drift | Delivery |
|---|---|
| 01 | Seated · slow · controlled frustration · not a productivity coach |
| 02 | Slightly amused · controlled sarcasm · faster pace · tools are not the enemy |
| 03 | Warmer · quieter · human · not CRM advice |
| 04 | Confident · sharper · copying as rational uncertainty |
| 05 | Curious · slightly disappointed · anti-collaboration tone forbidden |

Global:

* Speak as someone who has watched the pattern repeatedly.
* Do not rush toward solutions in the first fifty.
* Stay with the condition.
