## Four-part video movement

Use for each short observation:

1. **Show the behavior** — concrete, visible
2. **Name the normal explanation** — what owners assume
3. **Expose the deeper condition** — structural interpretation
4. **End with the reframe** — clearest version of the problem

Do not end every video with advice. Sometimes the strongest ending is simply the clearest version of the problem.
