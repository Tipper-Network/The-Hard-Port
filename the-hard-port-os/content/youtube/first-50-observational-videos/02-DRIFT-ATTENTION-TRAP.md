# Drift 02 — The Attention Trap

## Central thesis

Businesses use content, followers, trends, and paid reach as responses to uncertainty.

Attention is visible, measurable, and purchasable.

Understanding why people care, return, or disappear is slower and less comfortable.

## Filming objective

Record one continuous discussion about how marketing activity can become a substitute for understanding the business.

**Target raw recording length:** 20–30 minutes

**Expected output:**

- 3 foundational videos
- 8 short observations
- analytics and phone B-roll
- sarcastic but fair commentary

## Continuous recording prompt

Speak through this question:

What does a business do when it does not know why customers are not coming, returning, or caring?

Move through:

- posting more often;
- copying trends;
- checking views;
- boosting weak posts;
- changing formats;
- hiring creators;
- blaming algorithms;
- seeking followers;
- confusing reach with relationship;
- confusing activity with learning.

## FND-004 — I Noticed Why Businesses Keep Posting Even When It Is Not Working

### Primary observation

Posting is the easiest visible action available when the business does not understand what is wrong.

It creates the feeling of movement.

### Opening options

- "I noticed why businesses keep posting even when posting is not working."
- "When a business does not know what to change, it often changes the content."
- "Posting has become the default answer to business uncertainty."

### Speaking beats

- Sales feel slow or unpredictable.
- The business assumes it needs more visibility.
- More content is produced.
- Performance remains inconsistent.
- The response is even more content.
- Nobody stops to ask what has been learned.
- Content becomes emotional reassurance that the business is trying.

### Strong lines

- Posting can become a substitute for understanding.
- The business is not learning from the market. It is repeatedly asking the algorithm for a different answer.
- Activity feels safer than diagnosis.

### Closing options

- "Sometimes the business is not posting because it has something to say. It is posting because silence feels like failure."
- "More content cannot answer a question the business has not clearly asked."
- "The issue may not be that the business is unseen. It may be that what people see gives them no reason to remember."

## FND-005 — Businesses Think They Need More Followers

### Primary observation

A larger audience does not automatically create stronger customer relationships.

Businesses often ignore people already paying attention while chasing strangers.

### Opening options

- "Businesses think they need more followers."
- "A business can have thousands of followers and almost no relationship with them."
- "The obsession with audience size hides a more uncomfortable question: what happens after someone pays attention?"

### Speaking beats

- Followers are visible and easy to compare.
- Relationships are less visible.
- Acquisition is celebrated.
- Continuation is rarely measured.
- Existing customers become background.
- Conversation stops after the sale.
- Attention repeatedly enters and leaks away.

### Strong lines

- The account is growing while the relationship is restarting from zero every day.
- Businesses do not always need more attention. They need to stop wasting the attention they already received.
- A follower is not a relationship. It is an opportunity for one.

### Closing options

- "The business may not need more followers. It may need more repeat conversations."
- "The problem is not always audience size. It is what the business remembers after attention arrives."
- "Before finding another thousand people, notice the hundred who already raised their hands."

## FND-006 — Businesses Think Boosting Will Solve Their Visibility Problem

### Primary observation

Paid distribution can increase exposure.

It cannot make an unclear business meaningful, memorable, or trusted.

### Opening options

- "Businesses think boosting will solve their visibility problem."
- "Paying for more people to see something does not improve what they are seeing."
- "Boosting is useful. It is just frequently asked to solve the wrong problem."

### Speaking beats

- Organic performance feels weak.
- The business pays for reach.
- Numbers rise.
- Attention disappears when spending stops.
- Little learning is retained.
- The offer and customer path remain unchanged.
- The business purchases repeated exposure without developing recognition.

### Strong lines

- Boosting can buy distribution. It cannot buy meaning.
- Paid attention amplifies whatever is already there, including confusion.
- The business keeps renting visibility because it has not built memory.

### Closing options

- "More people seeing an unclear offer gives the business a larger unclear result."
- "The question is not only how many people saw the business. It is what remained after they saw it."
- "Visibility is useful. Being worth remembering is different."

- trend templates;
- refreshing views;
- a quiet physical shop while the account looks busy.

## Delivery direction

- Slightly amused.
- Controlled sarcasm.
- Faster pace than Drift 01.
- Do not attack content creators or paid media.
- The tools are not the enemy; the misdiagnosis is.
