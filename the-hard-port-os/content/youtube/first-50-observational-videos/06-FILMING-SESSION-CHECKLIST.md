# THP — Filming Session Checklist

Use this checklist for each drift.

## Before filming

### Content preparation

- Read the drift's central thesis.
- Read the continuous recording prompt.
- Mark the three strongest personal examples.
- Select one main closing line.
- Do not memorize the entire file.
- Keep the file nearby only as a directional guide.

### Technical preparation

- Charge camera and phone.
- Clear storage.
- Test microphone.
- Record 10 seconds and listen back.
- Lock exposure and focus.
- Remove background noise where possible.
- Place water nearby.
- Put phone on Do Not Disturb.

### Frame preparation

- Leave room for captions.
- Keep the background relevant but not distracting.
- Remove visible brand clutter unless intentional.
- Record a clean empty frame for editing.
- Capture 10 seconds of room tone.

## Recording order

### 1. Full drift

Record one uninterrupted monologue.

Do not stop for imperfect wording.

Pause, repeat the sentence, and continue.

### 2. Foundational hooks

Record each opening option separately.

Leave two seconds of silence before and after each take.

### 3. Strong lines

Record the strongest lines as direct-to-camera clips.

Use a slower pace.

### 4. Foundational endings

Record each closing option separately.

### 5. Supporting observations

Record every OBS entry as its own clip.

**Recommended length:** 8–25 seconds

### 6. B-roll

Capture at least:

- one wide shot;
- one medium shot;
- three close-ups;
- one phone or screen interaction;
- one silent reaction shot;
- one walking or movement shot;
- one environment shot.

## Performance checks

Before ending the session, confirm that you captured:

- one calm version;
- one more intense version;
- one version with a natural pause;
- one clean hook;
- one clean closing;
- all supporting observations.

## File naming

```text
DRIFT-01-RAW-01.mp4
FND-001-HOOK-01.mp4
FND-001-MAIN-01.mp4
FND-001-CLOSE-01.mp4
OBS-001-TAKE-01.mp4
BROLL-DRIFT-01-PHONE-01.mp4
```

## After filming

- Back up the raw footage.
- Do not delete imperfect takes.
- Write down the three strongest moments immediately.
- Note any new observation that emerged.
- Add new observations to the observation bank.
- Mark which foundational videos are ready for editing.
- Mark which clips need voice-over or B-roll.

## Editing principle

Do not force the raw drift into a single linear speech.

The drift is a source recording.

The final video may combine:

- one hook pickup;
- two sections from the free drift;
- one separately recorded strong line;
- B-roll;
- one closing pickup.

The final result should feel coherent, not necessarily chronologically identical to the recording.
