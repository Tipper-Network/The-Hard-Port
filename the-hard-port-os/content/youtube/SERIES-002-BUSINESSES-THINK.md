# Series Two — Businesses Think…

> **Format:** Foundational observational  
> **Working promise:** Expose the difference between the problem owners believe they have and the deeper problem underneath it.

Each video names what businesses **think** they need, then contrasts it with what the condition actually requires.

---

## Series Purpose

Move the viewer from misdiagnosis toward recognition:

* Audience size mistaken for relationship depth (F07)
* Acquisition mistaken for retention (F08)
* Content mistaken for clarity (F09)
* Branding mistaken for understanding (F10)
* Paid reach mistaken for meaningful offer (F11)
* Growth mistaken for stability (F12)
