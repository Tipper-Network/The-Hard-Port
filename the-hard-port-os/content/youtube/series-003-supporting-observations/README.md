**Format:** Supporting observation — short evidence, not a smaller lesson.

## Purpose

Short pieces extracted from everyday business behavior. They reinforce the worldview established by the foundational twelve (`F01`–`F12`).

They are **not**:

* Tutorials
* Standalone lessons
* Service promotion

They **are**:

* Evidence supporting a foundational claim
* Fuel for the first ~50 observe-first videos
* Source material for short-form cuts

---

## Publishing Rule

Between every foundational video, release approximately **three** supporting observations:

```text
F01 → OBS ×3 → F02 → OBS ×3 → … → F12
```

Target: ~36 supporting + 12 foundational ≈ **48 videos** in first cycle.
