# F10 — Businesses Think Branding Means Looking Professional

**Narrative stage:** Disconnection

## Believed problem

Business doesn't look credible or polished enough.

## Core contrast

**Looking established** · versus · **being understood**

## Core observation

A professional appearance cannot compensate for an unclear promise, inconsistent experience, or indistinguishable business.

## Underlying condition

Branding used to simulate evidence the business doesn't possess.

## Level 1 dimensions

H (Identity) · B (Customer understanding)

## Recognition CTA

List three things your brand claims. For each, write one customer behavior that proves it.
