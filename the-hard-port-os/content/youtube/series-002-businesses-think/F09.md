# F09 — Businesses Think They Have a Content Problem

**Narrative stage:** Misdiagnosis

## Believed problem

Posts aren't good enough, frequent enough, or on-trend enough.

## Core contrast

**Better content** · versus · **greater business clarity**

## Core observation

Their content may be inconsistent because the business itself has no clear message, priority, audience, or operating rhythm.

## Underlying condition

Content surface reflects internal disorder.

## Level 1 dimensions

H (Identity) · G (Evidence)

## Recognition CTA

Complete: "We help [who] achieve [what] unlike [alternative]." If you can't, that's the problem content can't fix.
