# F11 — Businesses Think Boosting Will Solve Their Visibility Problem

**Narrative stage:** Disconnection

## Believed problem

Not enough people see the offer.

## Core contrast

**Buying attention** · versus · **becoming worth remembering**

## Core observation

Paid reach can expose the business to more people, but it cannot make an unclear offer meaningful.

## Underlying condition

Paid visibility amplifies confusion when offer and demand are unclear.
