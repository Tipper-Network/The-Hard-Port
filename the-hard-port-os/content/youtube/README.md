## Institutional Position

The first fifty videos should primarily **observe**.

The first twelve are not disconnected topics, tutorials, educational modules, or service-promotion videos. They are THP's first public diagnostic layer — a developed expression of the observational strategy.

Do not introduce the full THP solution too early. The audience first needs to recognize the condition.

---

## Content Format Definitions

### Foundational observational video

A developed observation that establishes THP's worldview.

* Begins with visible business behavior
* Reveals the underlying structural condition
* One core observation per video
* Part of the twelve-video spine (`F01`–`F12`)
* **Not** classified as tutorial, educational module, or service promotion

### Supporting observation

A short piece of evidence reinforcing a foundational observation.

* Extracted from everyday business behavior
* Not a smaller lesson — evidence for the worldview
* Released between foundational videos (~3 per gap)
* Pulled from [`OBSERVATION-BANK.md`](./OBSERVATION-BANK.md)

### Instructional video

A later-stage video that helps an owner **respond** to a condition already identified.

* Comes after recognition is established
* May include evidence CTAs or readiness framing
* Not part of the first twelve

### Focus video

A deeper examination of one business condition, mechanism, or entity example.

* Single belief challenged and replaced (see THP-MEDIA-001 Format A)
* May use kinetic thesis or diagnostic breakdown structure
* Distinct from quick supporting observations

## Publishing Rhythm

Between every foundational video, release approximately three supporting observations:

```text
Main video → Supporting → Supporting → Supporting → Next main video
```

Target for first cycle:

* 12 foundational videos
* ~36 supporting observations
* ~48 videos total — consistent with "first fifty videos should primarily observe"

Observation pool (legacy, 45 entries): [`OBSERVATION-BANK.md`](./OBSERVATION-BANK.md) — superseded for first cycle by **OBS-001–038** in drift files.

## Narrative Progression

```text
Overwhelm → Reaction → Misdiagnosis → Disconnection → Recognition
```

| Stage | Foundational videos | What the viewer should feel |
|---|---|---|
| **Overwhelm** | F01 | The business lives inside the founder's head |
| **Reaction** | F02, F03 | Activity substitutes for understanding |
| **Misdiagnosis** | F04–F06, F07–F09 | Wrong problems pursued (identity, retention, followers, content) |
| **Disconnection** | F10–F11 | Surface fixes hide broken relationships and unclear offers |
| **Recognition** | F12 | More activity increases complexity, not stability |
