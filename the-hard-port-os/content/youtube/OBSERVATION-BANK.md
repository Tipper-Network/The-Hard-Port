# THP Observation Bank

## OBS-001

**Observation:** I noticed businesses respond faster to strangers than returning customers.

**Visible behavior:** DMs and comments from new audiences answered within minutes; repeat buyers wait days or get generic replies.

**Underlying condition:** Acquisition instinct dominates; retention treated as optional courtesy.

**Relevant business level:** L1

**Possible format:** Short

**Related foundational video:** F05

**Status:** Selected
