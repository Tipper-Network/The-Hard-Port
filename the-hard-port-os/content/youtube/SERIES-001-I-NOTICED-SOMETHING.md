# Series One — I Noticed Something…

> **Format:** Foundational observational  
> **Working promise:** Begin with a visible business behavior. Reveal the underlying structural problem.

These videos do not open with theory. They open with something the owner has **seen** — in their business or a neighbor's — and name what that behavior actually indicates.

---

## Series Purpose

Expose Level 1 structural conditions through recognizable daily behavior:

* Founder dependency and operational memory (F01)
* Activity as substitute for understanding (F02)
* Transaction rewards mistaken for relationship (F03)
* Identity confusion expressed as imitation (F04)
* Retention failure after first purchase (F05)
* Partnership theater without operating structure (F06)

## Videos

### F01 — I Noticed Why Small Businesses Never Have Enough Time

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **File** | [`series-001-i-noticed-something/F01.md`](./series-001-i-noticed-something/F01.md) |
| **Narrative stage** | Overwhelm |
| **Visible behavior** | Founder is always working; calendar full; no time to improve systems |
| **Core observation** | A business does not consume all your time because it is growing. It consumes all your time because too much of it still lives inside your head. |
| **Underlying condition** | Founder dependency; business knowledge not externalized into repeatable systems |
| **Level** | L1 — F (Founder dependency), E (Operations) |
| **Contrast** | Busy because growing · vs · busy because the business cannot run without you |
| **Status** | `not_started` |

## Appropriate CTAs (F01–F06)

Recognition and self-observation only. Examples:

* "Which part of this have you seen in your business?"
* "Write down one thing only you remember about how customers return."
* "Name the last activity you increased when results didn't improve."

No application CTA. No THP service explanation required in early videos — link only when description supports institutional context.
