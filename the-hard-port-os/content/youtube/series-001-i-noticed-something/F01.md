# F01 — I Noticed Why Small Businesses Never Have Enough Time

**Narrative stage:** Overwhelm

## Visible behavior

The owner is always working. Calendar full. No time to improve systems — only to react.

## Core observation

> A business does not consume all your time because it is growing.  
> It consumes all your time because too much of it still lives inside your head.

## Underlying condition

The business depends on the founder remembering, deciding, supervising, and rescuing everything. Operational knowledge is not externalized.

## Level 1 dimensions

F (Founder dependency) · E (Operations)

## Recognition CTA

Write down three decisions only you made this week that no system recorded.
