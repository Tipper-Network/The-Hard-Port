# F02 — I Noticed Why Businesses Keep Posting Even When It Is Not Working

**Narrative stage:** Reaction

## Visible behavior

Posting continues — often increases — when sales stay flat or unclear.

## Core observation

> Content becomes a substitute for understanding the business.

Posting has become the default response to uncertainty. When businesses do not know why customers come, return, or disappear, they publish more content.

## Underlying condition

Activity mistaken for learning; uncertainty managed through visibility.

## Level 1 dimensions

A (Demand) · G (Evidence and decision-making)

## Recognition CTA

Name the business result your last ten posts were supposed to produce. What changed?
