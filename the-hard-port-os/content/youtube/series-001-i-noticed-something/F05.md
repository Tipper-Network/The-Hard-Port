# F05 — I Noticed Why Customers Stop Coming Back

**Narrative stage:** Misdiagnosis

## Visible behavior

Strong first purchase; silence afterward. Owner surprised customers didn't return.

## Core observation

> The customer did not necessarily reject the business. The relationship simply disappeared after the transaction.

Many businesses concentrate on the first purchase but have no deliberate process for remembering, recognizing, or continuing the customer relationship.

## Underlying condition

Retention improvised; relationship discontinuity treated as rejection.

## Level 1 dimensions

C (Retention) · B (Customer understanding)

## Recognition CTA

Pick your last five customers. Who followed up with whom — and when did contact stop?
