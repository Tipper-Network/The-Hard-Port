# F06 — I Noticed Why Partnerships Between Small Businesses Fail

**Narrative stage:** Misdiagnosis

## Visible behavior

Cross-promotions, shout-outs, joint posts — enthusiasm without measurable outcome.

## Core observation

> Two businesses mentioning each other is not a partnership.

Partnerships frequently begin with enthusiasm rather than a shared objective, defined contribution, audience fit, or measurement system.

## Underlying condition

Social collaboration theater without operating structure.

## Level 1 dimensions

H (Position) · C (Community) · Other (partnership design)

## Recognition CTA

Name your last "partnership." What was the shared objective and how was it measured?
