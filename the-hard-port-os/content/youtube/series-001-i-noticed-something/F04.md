# F04 — I Noticed Why Local Businesses Copy Each Other

**Narrative stage:** Misdiagnosis

## Visible behavior

Same promotions, aesthetics, offers, and language as competitors on the same street or feed.

## Core observation

> Copying is often not a creativity problem. It is an identity problem.

Businesses copy competitors because their own identity, customer understanding, and strategic direction are unclear.

## Underlying condition

Imitation substitutes for position; market defined by peers not buyers.

## Level 1 dimensions

H (Identity and market position)

## Recognition CTA

Name one thing you copied from a competitor in the last six months. What were you unsure about when you copied it?
