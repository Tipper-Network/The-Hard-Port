# F03 — I Noticed Why Loyalty Programs Do Not Create Loyalty

**Narrative stage:** Reaction

## Visible behavior

Points, punch cards, discount codes — customers return when incentivized, not necessarily when aligned.

## Core observation

> A customer returning for a discount is not the same as a customer belonging to the business.

Points and discounts can reward transactions, but they do not necessarily create relationships.

## Underlying condition

Transaction repetition confused with relationship; no belonging infrastructure.

## Level 1 dimensions

C (Retention and community)

## Recognition CTA

List customers who returned only when offered a discount. What do they have in common?
