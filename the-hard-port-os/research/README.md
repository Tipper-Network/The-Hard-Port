# Research

All questions begin here. Research is working material: questions, observations, hypotheses, principles, contradictions, concepts, and experiments that may eventually improve systems and Canon synthesis.

## Current Work

- [Current Research Agenda](./CURRENT_RESEARCH_AGENDA.md)
- [Research Program 001 — Systems, Identity, and Self-Authorship](./RESEARCH-PROGRAM-001.md)

## Rule

Research does not become canonical because it is interesting. It advances through the evidence, RFC, decision, system, and Canon gates defined in `../KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## What Belongs Here

Questions, programs, hypotheses, experiments, observations, contradictions, findings, principles, and concepts under investigation.

## What Does Not Belong Here

Accepted decisions, current system specifications, procedures, Canon chapters, or source-free conclusions.

## How Documents Enter

Material enters with a defined research role, provenance, owner, scope, and next action.

## How Documents Leave or Become Superseded

Research may be contested, merged, retired, or used by an RFC and Canon synthesis. It remains in place as evidence and provenance.

## Related Folders

- `../rfcs/` proposes changes based on research.
- `../decisions/` records accepted choices.
- `../systems/` defines operational reality.
- `../canon/` synthesizes accepted knowledge.
- `../archive/` preserves obsolete or session-specific sources.
