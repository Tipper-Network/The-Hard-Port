# How THP Works With Level 1 Businesses

> Public service explanation — publish at `/work-with-us`.  
> Not a complete website. Answers the minimum questions before application.

## Who we help

Established **online or online-dependent microbusinesses** that:

- Have a defined offer and paying customers
- Are operating, not pre-launch
- Are stuck in **Level 1 — established but surviving**
- Have tried content, branding, or promotion without building a stable foundation

See [`THP-SMB-001-FIRST-ONBOARDER`](../../docs/04-market/first-onboarder/THP-SMB-001-FIRST-ONBOARDER.md).

## What condition you're probably in

You have customers. You have activity. But the business still runs on **reaction, hope, and founder effort** — not on clear evidence, repeatable delivery, or a path to stability.

Common patterns:

- Revenue arrives unpredictably
- Marketing activity doesn't connect to what customers actually buy
- The founder is the bottleneck for decisions and delivery
- You can't explain what's working vs what's noise

That's Level 1. It's not failure. It's **missing foundation**.

## What THP does

THP helps Level 1 businesses build the **Level 2 operating foundation** — the conditions required to prepare for responsible growth.

Our work follows a structured sequence:

1. **Application** — you describe your business honestly
2. **Qualification** — we decide if THP should diagnose you
3. **Business Reality Diagnostic** (paid) — evidence-based assessment of your actual condition
4. **Findings** — we show you what the evidence supports
5. **Intervention** (optional, separate agreement) — structured work to establish stability across eight phases

THP is **not** a content calendar, rebrand, or ad-buying service. Execution (content, identity, workflows) happens **inside** the intervention when a diagnosed constraint requires it.

## What we do not promise

- Guaranteed sales, profit, or virality
- That every applicant is accepted
- That every client reaches Level 2
- A marketing audit or "social media fix"
- That posting more content solves a structural problem

## What happens after you apply

1. We review your application (self-reported — not yet verified).
2. If sufficient, we invite qualification review.
3. Qualification determines whether we proceed to the **paid diagnostic**.
4. The diagnostic produces findings — you may purchase it **without** committing to intervention.
5. If both parties agree, we propose the Level 1-to-Level 2 Stability Intervention under a separate scope and payment.

**Applying does not guarantee acceptance, diagnosis, or a commercial engagement.**

## Why the diagnostic is different from a marketing audit

| Marketing audit | Business Reality Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| Reviews ads, content, creative | Reviews business condition across eight dimensions |
| Optimizes visibility | Identifies primary constraint to stability |
| Often leads to more posting | Often leads to stopping unsupported actions |
| Sales-oriented | Professional assessment — valuable even if you don't continue |

## Capacity (field-validation period)

During our first field-validation cycle, THP accepts **one active diagnostic or intervention client at a time**. This protects quality and learning.
