# Minimum Commercial Readiness — Business Reality Diagnostic

> **Working operational summary — not final pricing.** Complete before accepting payment from the first applicant.  
> Offer architecture: [`THP-SMB-SERVICE-001`](../../knowledge/smb-service/THP-SMB-SERVICE-001-offer-architecture.md)

## Service name

**Business Reality Diagnostic**

## What it includes

- Qualification review (if not already completed)
- Diagnostic preparation and evidence request
- Level 1 diagnostic across dimensions A–H (field-tested interview)
- Evidence inventory and status classification
- Level 1 classification and Level 2 gap assessment
- Primary-constraint hypothesis and immediate-risk register
- Findings presentation (live session + written summary)
- Recommendation on whether intervention is appropriate

## What it excludes

- Level 1-to-Level 2 Stability Intervention
- Branding, content production, or campaign execution
- Guaranteed revenue, sales, or follower outcomes
- Legal, tax, or accounting services
- Unlimited revision cycles or open-ended consulting
- Tipper setup or software implementation

## Why it is not a marketing audit

The diagnostic examines **business condition and maturity evidence** — not ad performance, content calendars, or creative quality in isolation.
