# Skill — Positioning / Smallest Viable Audience (Godin procedure)

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` and `godin-remarkable` first.

Use this before writing identity-defining copy (hero lines, "who we're for" sections, new
campaign angles) — not for routine section edits.

## Procedure

1. **Write the widest possible version of the audience first** ("small business owners"), then
   narrow it at least twice more: what kind of small business owner, in what specific situation,
   feeling what specific way right now. Stop narrowing only when the description could not
   apply to two different businesses in the same city.
2. **Write the explicit exclusion.** Who is this specifically not for? State it as plainly as
   the inclusion. Exclusion is not the enemy of conversion here — it's what makes the included
   group lean in harder.
3. **Find the identity line, not the feature line.** Draft one sentence that describes who the
   reader is (or wants to become) rather than what the agency does. Test it against: "would a
   reader want to be the kind of person this sentence describes?"
4. **Check against the existing letter and hero copy** for consistency — a new campaign's
   audience definition should feel like the same person the rest of the site is already talking
   to, not a new segment bolted on.
5. Run the result through `aggression-calibration` — narrowing an audience aggressively is
   fine; insulting people outside the audience for existing is not the goal.
