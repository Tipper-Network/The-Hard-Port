# Skill — Offer Construction (Hormozi Grand Slam Offer procedure)

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` and `THP-SMB-SERVICE-001-offer-architecture.md` first.
Use the `hormozi-value` instinct for small edits to existing offers.

Use this when building a new commercial mechanic — diagnostic scope, intervention milestones,
capacity rules, continuation offers — **not** for legacy Port/Sea/Ocean package grids or
free-slot mechanics (retired from public funnel).

## Procedure

1. **Name the dream outcome in the reader's own words** — Level 2 stability, evidence-based
   direction, not "brand visibility" or "more content."
2. **List every believable proof point** — real numbers only, per `hormozi-value`. Lean on
   honesty-as-proof when the list is thin.
3. **List every friction point** — vague agency deliverables, fake audits, acceptance assumed,
   months of posting without diagnosis.
4. **Strip or reverse each friction point** in the offer structure: fixed-scope diagnostic,
   named phases, visible qualification gate, stated what THP will **not** do.
5. **Stack, don't just discount** — e.g. diagnostic credit toward intervention (SERVICE-001
   working default: 50% credit within validity period), not "free strategy call."
6. **Write names and one-liners last** — after structure is settled, so language reflects real
   commercial difference (application vs diagnostic vs intervention), not budget tiers.
7. Run through `.cursor/rules/01-copywriting-standards.mdc` and `aggression-calibration`
   before shipping to web.
