# Skill — Guarantee / Risk Reversal Design

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` and `THP-SMB-SERVICE-001` first.

A guarantee's job is to move risk from the buyer to the seller so the decision gets easier, not
to be a marketing bullet point. Follow this procedure any time one is added or changed.

## Procedure

1. Identify the single biggest reason this specific reader (see `small-business-psychology`)
   hesitates to commit — usually: "I've paid for this before and gotten nothing."
2. Design the guarantee to directly cancel that fear, not a generic fear. For SERVICE-001,
   structural risk reversal includes: fixed-scope diagnostic, findings valuable without
   intervention, diagnostic fee credit toward intervention (working default 50%), and explicit
   **what THP does not promise** — not legacy free-slot mechanics.
3. State the guarantee in one plain sentence, no legal hedging language, no asterisks doing the
   real work in fine print — tone matches the site, not terms-of-service.
4. Make sure Hard Port can deliver on it every time. A broken guarantee is worse than none.
5. Place near the point of decision (`/work-with-us`, `/apply`, capacity section) — not buried
   in FAQ where it does no work.

## SERVICE-001 boundary

THP promises process integrity and honest interpretation — not sales, profit, or Level 2
guarantees. Risk reversal must not slide into outcome guarantees the commercial model forbids.
