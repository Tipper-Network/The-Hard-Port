# Instinct — The Hormozi Value Checks

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` first.

Value = (Dream Outcome x Belief) / (Time Delay x Effort). This file is the fast gut-check
version; `offer-construction` skill is the slow, full procedure for building a new offer from
scratch. Run this instinct anytime an offer, price, or guarantee is touched, even in a small edit.

## Quick checks

- **Believability over size.** A big promised outcome with weak believability converts worse
  than a smaller promise this specific market actually believes. For small business owners
  already burned by agencies, believability is the scarcer resource — don't spend copy
  inflating the outcome at the expense of the reader trusting it's real.
- **Effort is often emotional, not just logistical.** For this market, "effort" includes having
  to admit the current approach isn't working, not just filling out a form. Anywhere the offer
  can absorb that emotional effort for the reader (e.g. "we call you so you don't feel like
  you're asking for help"), that's worth more than shaving a form field.
- **Real scarcity only.** "2 businesses a month, we pick" works because it's a real operational
  constraint, not a countdown timer. If a scarcity mechanic can't be defended as literally true,
  it doesn't belong on this site — this market's skepticism will catch a fake deadline instantly
  and it will cost more trust than the urgency was worth.
- **Guarantees reduce effort-of-decision, not just risk.** Anywhere a new price point or tier is
  added, check whether the risk of a wrong decision has been visibly reduced for the reader, not
  just whether the price is justified.
