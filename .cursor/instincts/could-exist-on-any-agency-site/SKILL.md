# Instinct — Could This Exist on Any Other Agency's Website?

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` and `.cursor/rules/01-copywriting-standards.mdc` first.

Before shipping any new section, ask: could this sentence, component, or layout be copy-pasted
onto a generic marketing agency site with just the logo swapped? If yes, it's not finished.

## SERVICE-001-specific smells (instant fail)

- Three-tier pricing grid with feature checkmarks
- "Free strategy call" / "free audit" / "free slot" as the hook
- "We handle your social media" as the primary promise
- Boosting vs campaigns as the core mechanism section
- Testimonial carousel with stock praise
- "Compare plans" / "Choose your package"

## Passes the test

- Level 1 condition language, diagnostic boundaries, qualification gatekeeping
- Anti-testimonial honesty ($0 vs $270k)
- One-client capacity stated as operational fact
- Application → diagnostic → intervention ladder without agency tier names

Fastest gut-check for corporate drift — faster than scanning the banned-words list alone.
