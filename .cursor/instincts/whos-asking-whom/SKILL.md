# Instinct — Who's Asking Whom?

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` first.

The brand posture is: the client is auditioning for us, not the other way around. Any copy or
UX flow that reads like we're trying to convince, persuade, or win the visitor over has the
power dynamic backwards. Reframe it as us deciding whether they qualify.
