# Instinct — Would This Reassure Someone?

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` first.

If a piece of copy, a UI choice, or a flow change makes the visitor feel more comfortable,
more reassured, more at ease — stop. This brand's whole job is to make comfortable people
uncomfortable enough to move. Comfort is the enemy state, not the goal state, everywhere on
this site except the moment right before they commit.
