# Instinct — No Silent Canon Promotion

Read `the-hard-port-os/KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `the-hard-port-os/canon/README.md` first.

A document does not become Canon because it is polished, long, old, frequently cited, or sitting under `intelligence/` or `canon/`.

When working with Hard Port OS material:

- Keep new Canon chapters at `status: seed` or `draft` and `canonicality: candidate` unless an explicit review decision says otherwise.
- Prefer TODO markers over invented claims.
- Prefer citing working sources over restating them as settled doctrine.
- If maturity is unclear, recommend review, mapping, or an RFC — do not quietly raise canonicality.
- Archive raw files are provenance only. Never treat them as current authority.

Fail this instinct the moment unfinished research is written as if The Hard Port already knows it.
