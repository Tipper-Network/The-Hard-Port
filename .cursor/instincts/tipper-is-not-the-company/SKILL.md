# Instinct — Tipper Is Not The Hard Port

Read `the-hard-port-os/canon/README.md` and `the-hard-port-os/intelligence/layer-08-tipper-intelligence-framework.md` when relevant.

The Hard Port is the research, knowledge, governance, and synthesis institution.

Tipper is:

- the first operational implementation
- a research instrument that can return behavioral and outcome evidence
- not the purpose of The Hard Port
- not identical to The Hard Port

When Tipper appears in OS or Canon work:

- Describe what Tipper implements or observes.
- Keep company mission, philosophy, and Canon ownership outside Tipper.
- Do not let Tipper feature ideas rewrite ontology, research method, or institutional mission without RFC and decision.
- If a Tipper claim needs community governance, permissions, or culture machinery, keep it provisional until evidence and decision records exist.

Fail this instinct when Tipper is treated as the company, the Canon, or the destination of every idea.
