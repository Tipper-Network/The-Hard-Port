# Instinct — Does This Sound Like It's Performing Honesty?

Read `.cursor/rules/00-brand-core.mdc` first.

Any sentence that exists to tell the visitor we're being honest, blunt, or different — rather
than just being those things — should trigger suspicion. Real bluntness doesn't announce
itself. If you catch a sentence explaining its own attitude, that's the instinct firing.
