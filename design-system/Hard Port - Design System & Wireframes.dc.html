Live Site Audit — thehardport.com today
Flag — Order broken: Scarcity before Offer Stack
"2 free slots a month" currently sits before the pricing ladder. The brief's ladder puts Offer Stack (6) ahead of Scarcity (7) — climb the ladder first, then reveal the gate.
Flag — Off-ladder "Who We Are" block before FAQ
An About/"Who We Are" section sits between the pricing table and the FAQ. It isn't one of the 9 rungs, and it cools the room right where intensity should be climbing toward objection-handling and last call.
Flag — Banned words in the feature-comparison table
"Compare Features," "Detailed Breakdown," and "Expand each feature" all appear verbatim on the live table — all three are explicitly on the brief's banned list.
Flag — Cold nav skips the ladder
Top nav exposes "Pricing" and "Who We Are" as jump links from second one. A cold visitor can skip agitation, mechanism, and proof entirely — directly against "nothing mid-page should resolve tension early."
Note — two unlabeled sections need a home
A "how it works" 4-step block and a "gap: where you are / where you want to be" block currently float between named rungs. Not wrong content — fold them explicitly into Mechanism (4) and Offer Stack (6) rather than leaving them as unnamed extra sections.
Extend later, don't build now — collapse "Pricing" and "Who We Are" out of the cold top nav into the footer only. Testing-ground, teaching, and Tipper-intro sites aren't designed this pass — this IA keeps nav/footer slots open for them without restructuring the ladder.
